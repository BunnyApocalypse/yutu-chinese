# Words: adding them, scripts and readings

## Adding words

- **Words are added in "+ Add to deck"** (`docs/decks.md`), one at a time, as a list pasted or chosen as a file, or as a sentence. A device starts with none.
- **A word is its traditional characters plus its pinyin** (the ID). Both can be corrected in its pop-up panel, which moves its cards and reviews (`renameWord`); so can the meaning, simplified form and Taiwan reading. The same characters with another reading are a different word.
- **Typing characters fills in the form by itself** (`autoFill` in `src/addWordForm.js`; the owner, 2026-10-14: tapping was a step too many): CC-CEDICT's entries are listed under the box, one per reading, names last, and the one with the usual reading fills in the fields: the first the HSK 3.0 list gives (by level), else TOCFL's, else the dictionary's first (alphabetical, so 行 would be háng). Tapping another entry fills in that one, and it stays (`entryTapped`). A field the learner typed in is never overwritten (`autoValues`); with both scripts asked, the other box is filled too. Everything stays editable: the owner words the meaning, since writing prompts show only it.
- **How much meaning is proposed** (`proposeMeaning`, the "Dictionary meanings" setting `meaningDetail`; the owner, 2026-10-13): `first`, `everyday` (default: every meaning except those labeled with a specialist field, `SPECIALIST_FIELDS`, unless it has only those), or `all`. CC-CEDICT separates near-synonyms with slashes as often as semicolons, so cutting at the first slash lost shades of meaning.
- **By pinyin** (2026-10-15: a beginner without a Chinese keyboard couldn't add a word): the box also takes `nihao`, `ni hao`, `ni3hao3`, `nǐhǎo` (`v` or `ü`), told from characters by `readTypedPinyin` in `src/dictionary.js`, and lists words read so (`lookUpByPinyin`; `latinSuggestions` in `src/addWordForm.js`). Nothing is filled in until one is tapped. Order (agreed with the owner): exact readings before readings that only begin so (from two letters, `zhongg` → 中国); typed tones, even on some syllables, keep only words with them (unless none); then HSK 3.0 and TOCFL words in list order, names, variants (a word in no list with the same reading and meanings as an earlier one; a listed word never is, so 辭典 stays), and the rest by their rarest character (义 before 㑊). Eight shown, "Show more" adds eight, with advice to type a tone if none; words already yours are marked "in your words". The index is built at the first pinyin typed (about 0.35 s). Not done: `nu` for 女 nǚ; pinyin in Add a sentence or the pop-up panel.
- **By English meaning** (tester 2, wanting "piano"; the owner, 2026-10-18): letters with no Chinese (`isLatin`) list exact readings, then words with that meaning (`lookUpByMeaning`, from three letters), then readings that only begin so, each once (`lookUpByLetters` in `src/dictionary.js`, also used by the Words tab: "piano" begins pián'ǒu, and those had come before 鋼琴). The boxes read "Word: 漢字, pinyin or English" and "Traditional 繁體, pinyin or English". Pinyin or English left in the box is not added. Nothing found: "No word in the dictionary is read that way or means that."
- **A word already there under any reading is pointed out** ("Already in your words"), and a list counts new words sharing characters with an existing one (`wordsWithSameCharacters`): dictionaries disagree on neutral tones (péngyou, péngyǒu), and the ID includes the pinyin.
- **Pinyin is stored tidied** (`tidyPinyin` in `src/words.js`): tone numbers, no spaces or hyphens, `v` for ü, an apostrophe only before a later syllable starting with a, e or o, several readings joined by ", ". The dictionary is built with the same function, so IDs match. Tone marks aren't accepted as input.
- **Lists are read as TSV or CSV** (a line with a tab is TSV). Skritter's CSV doesn't quote fields with commas, so `columnsFromCsv` works out which fields are pinyin and meaning. Both of the owner's exports read to 2,298 words; the 40 lines left out are zhuyin and patterns like "yue4 ... yue4".
- **New words go to the end of the queue** (`order` one above the highest); "Learn this word next" gives an `order` below every other, so it can be negative.
- **"Edit word" on Review's answer page** (the owner, 2026-10-13) opens the pop-up panel (`edit(word)` in `src/wordsPanel.js`) while the grades show. A changed meaning alone keeps the answer up, still to grade (`update(word)` in `src/recallCard.js`, `src/toneFlow.js`); any other change shows the card again unanswered; a removed word brings the next card. Not in Free practice or before the answer.
- **The pop-up panel closes only after the app has read the changed word** (`afterChange` awaits `onChange`; a `<dialog>`'s "close" event comes a moment after it closes, which `edit()` waits for). After any change from the Decks tab, Review and Free practice read the words afresh.
- **The list on screen shows at most 200 rows** (`MAX_ROWS`); beyond that, search. ("Show more" is on the roadmap.)

## The dictionary and the Words tab's search

- **The dictionary is a separate 8.4 MB file, fetched only when a word is about to be typed** (`src/dictionaryLoader.js`, through a `?url` import so Vite keeps it out of the program), indexed by word and taken apart only on lookup. Without it, fields are filled in by hand. It is saved for offline use (`docs/offline.md`).
- **The Words tab searches the dictionary too** (the owner, 2026-10-15): while something is searched, dictionary words not among yours are listed under yours, "In the dictionary" (`dictionary-part`, `searchDictionary` in `src/wordsPanel.js`), ten at first, "Show more" adding twenty. Unless the switch "Dictionary results" is off (`dictionary-results`, on to begin with, not remembered; it was a checkbox "My words only" until tester 2 took it for broken, 2026-10-18), Show is "All words", and nothing is being selected.
- Found by characters (`lookUpByCharacters`: written so, then longer words beginning so, 老 → 老師), pinyin, and English meaning (`lookUpByMeaning`: a whole meaning or gloss first, "eat" in "to eat; to consume"; then a whole word; then a word's start, "teach" → "teacher"; "to", "a", "the" and labels ignored; then list order, names, variants), then readings that only begin so, each word once.
- **Searched once typing pauses** (200 ms): every meaning takes 20 to 50 ms on the PC, several times that on an old phone (the owner asked, 2026-10-15). The same text again is searched at once. A tap on a row opens the word's page (`openEntry`), where it is added (`docs/word-page.md`). On a deck's page the search is of the deck's words only.

## Fitting the interface to what is learned

- **Script:** "Traditional only", "Simplified only", "Both, cards in traditional", "Both, cards in simplified" (`script` plus `bothScripts`). `script` is what cards are shown and written in, unless the word has its own; `bothScripts` only decides what the Decks tab asks for and shows.
- **Pronunciation:** "Mainland China (普通话)", "Taiwan (國語)", "Accept both" (`pronunciation`: `mainlandOnly`, `taiwan`, `mainland`; the owner renamed them on 2026-10-14 and 15). With Taiwan, the mainland reading is in small print beside Taiwan's, since words are filed under it. The reading boxes are named by region, Taiwan's first when chosen (`showReadingBoxes`); with Mainland only, one box, "Pinyin". **There is no "Taiwan only"**: `pinyin` is the mainland reading and part of the ID.
- **A new device starts simplified only, mainland only** (the owner, 2026-10-13: a "Taiwan reading" box would confuse a beginner); the introduction asks at once. Older devices keep the old defaults (`keepOldDefaults` in `src/main.js`: both scripts, cards in traditional, mainland first). The browser tests choose those old defaults when they start (`openFreePractice` in the harness).
- **One script learned:** one characters box, "Word or phrase 漢字" (or 汉字); rows, titles and messages in that script only (`charactersShown` in `src/words.js`); no Simplified box for a traditional-only learner. Search matches both.
- **Every word stores both scripts.** A word typed in one gets the other from the dictionary (`completeForms` in `src/dictionaryLoader.js`, `otherScriptForm` in `src/dictionary.js`): the tapped entry, else the entry with the same characters and reading (头发 fà → 頭髮, not 頭發), else the dictionary's form if its entries agree, else as typed. So a simplified-only word missing from the dictionary has its simplified form as its ID, until corrected in the panel.
- **A list may have one column of characters** (characters, pinyin, meaning); `readWordList` tells by the second column being pinyin (or pinyin with tone marks, or having no characters: a meaning), and the other script is looked up, so checking a list fetches the dictionary first.
- **What a list leaves out is filled in from the dictionary** (tester 4, 2026-10-21, a list of words one per line): a line of characters alone gets the usual reading (`likeliestEntry`) and its proposed meaning; characters and pinyin get the meaning of the word read so; pinyin with tone marks ("nǐ hǎo") is matched to the dictionary's reading (`entryFor` in `src/dictionaryLoader.js`, `fillIn` of `readWordList`). A meaning the list gives is kept. A word not in the dictionary is reported ("not in the dictionary, so its pinyin and meaning are needed").
- **Mainland only:** no Taiwan box, no "Taiwan:" on cards (`readingsFor` gives no other reading), and tone cards don't accept the Taiwan tone (`toneAnswers`' `regional`); the neutral variant still is. What is out of sight is kept: a hidden simplified form or Taiwan reading survives saving, and a list's Taiwan column is stored whatever the setting.
- **A word can have its own script** (`cardScript`: `traditional`, `simplified`, or absent for "As in Settings"; the owner, 2026-10-13), for a mixed deck. "Cards in" is in the add form (`scriptAsked`) and the panel, shown only with a "Both" setting. **`scriptFor(word, settings)` is the one place that decides a word's script.** A card in the other script says so above it in gray, "simp. 简体" or "trad. 繁體" (`scriptFlagFor`, `script-flag`). A list can't set it.

## Taiwan and mainland pronunciation

- **`pinyin` is the mainland reading; `pinyinTaiwan`, if present, the Taiwan one** (a list's optional fifth column). `pronunciation` decides which is the answer and expected on tone cards; the other is shown beside it, labeled. `readingsFor` in `src/tones.js` is the one place that decides this.
- **Shown readings are never guessed** (the owner): a Taiwan reading comes only from the word's own column or a whole-word "Taiwan pr." note in CC-CEDICT (`taiwanWords`). Readings recalled from memory turned out wrong for several words: always check against Taiwan's MOE dictionary (moedict.tw).
- **Accepted tones are wider than shown readings** (`toneAnswers`): as a *variant*, the other region's tone; a tone CC-CEDICT links to the listed one as a mainland and Taiwan reading of the character; and the character's full tone where the list says neutral. These accept some things neither standard says (yìnggāi for 應該), so a variant answer is pointed out on the card with the listed reading.
- **A neutral tone where the list has a full one** (the owner, 2026-10-13): a pointed-out variant where CC-CEDICT also gives the word a neutral reading (`neutralWords`, 122 words: 東西 dōngxi, 知道); simply right where the owner added it in "Readings also accepted on tone cards" (`pinyinAlso`, not part of the ID, any reading, used only by tone cards and search). Elsewhere neutral is wrong.
- **The tone table** `data/cedict/tones.json` is built by `scripts/build-tone-table.js`, run by hand (the only script using the internet); CC BY-SA 4.0. Loaded with a dynamic `import()`; if it fails, tones are judged against the word list alone.
