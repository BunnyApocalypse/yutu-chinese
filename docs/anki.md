# Importing from Anki

Roadmap item 5, built on 2026-10-19, for learners moving from Anki. Decided with the owner that day, from Claude's proposals: the libraries, keeping schedules exactly, the learner confirming the guesses, writing left to the learner, tone cards following pinyin, the most-reviewed source winning, and `.colpkg` accepted too.

## What it does

- **Where:** on the Decks tab's front page the button beside the heading reads "+ New / import deck", and its panel has "Import a deck from Anki" under the name box (`anki-import-button`, which closes that panel; the owner, 2026-10-19). The introduction's starting level offers "A deck from Anki" too, which ends in Decks with the import open. On a deck's page the button is "+ New deck" and the link is hidden, since the import makes decks of its own. It opens a pop-up (`anki-dialog`) in three steps: choose a file; check the guesses; what was brought over.
- **Files:** a deck export (`.apkg`) or a whole collection (`.colpkg`), from any Anki version: `collection.anki21b` (current Anki, zstd-compressed, schema 18), `collection.anki21` ("support older Anki versions" ticked, schema 11) or `collection.anki2` (Anki 2.0; a current Anki puts a stand-in there too, so it is read last). Audio and pictures are not read.
- **Decks to bring:** a checkbox per Anki deck that has cards. Anki's decks become the app's, nested as in Anki ("Chinese::HSK 1" is HSK 1 inside Chinese); a deck with the same name in the same place is reused, so a second import makes no new decks. Ready-made decks are never used.
- **The check step, per note type: a summary first** (the owner, 2026-10-21, who as a user didn't understand the open lists and checkboxes; chosen from two drafts over "the same layout, clearer words"): "Each note becomes a word, like:" with the first note as the import would make it ("愛 ài · to love", on a line of its own), "Your Anki reviews count for: Meaning, Pinyin and Tone.", and a hint for the rest ("Writing starts new: a few words a day in Review, with a lesson."); without review history in the file, only the first line. Under it **"Change this"** (`.anki-change`, a fold-out closed at first, opened by itself when the first note would be left out) holds the choices below in two parts, each with a heading and what it asks: "Where the notes keep each part of the word" (the lists) and "What your Anki reviews practiced" (the checkboxes; "Reviewing Anki's “Card 1” (4995 cards, showing Simplified, Audio, Definitions on the front), you practiced:"). **Rows only for the templates the guesses use** (the owner, 2026-10-21: Ch_book1 showed 23 rows of four): the others, with cards but guessed as nothing, are named on one line after them, "Not brought over: Explanation, Meaning, Structure, …" (`.anki-unused`), with a quiet link "Show their checkboxes" ("its" for one) that shows their rows there and moves focus to the first box. Split by the guesses, not the ticks, so a row doesn't vanish when unticked; if no template is guessed, every row shows. Ch_book1 now shows 1 row for its grammar notes and 2 for its words. The summary follows every change. At the end, "Looks right? Press Import."
- **Which field is which**, per note type: three lists (Characters, Pinyin, Meaning), each field shown with the first note's text (`guessRoles`). A field's score for a role is (fit / filled + fit / sampled) / 2, if at least half its filled notes fit, so a field filled in a few notes doesn't beat one filled in all (Radical, not Simplified; Pinyin, not Examples Pinyin); a name with a hint in it adds 0.1, a name that is exactly a hint (`EXACT_NAME_HINTS`: "Pinyin", "Meaning", "Characters") 0.2, and an empty field's name 0.6. A field is pinyin only if each comma-separated reading has a tone and is made of Mandarin syllables (`isPinyin`, with `isSyllables` and `SYLLABLES` in `src/pinyinSyllables.js`: the 420 syllables with v for ü, the interjections, r alone, and an r after any syllable), so "HSK 1", "Top 500" and Vietnamese "nhất" aren't. A note type without a field of characters is left out. Where there is no pinyin field and the meaning's first line is pinyin (Anki's Basic with "lǎoshī<br>teacher" on the back), that line is the pinyin.
- **What each card counts for**, per template: checkboxes Meaning, Pinyin, Tone, Writing (`guessCardTypes`); the fields a template shows are named once each (`fieldsInTemplate`), though a drawing script may use one many times. Characters on the front → Meaning, and Pinyin and Tone if the reading is only on the back. Meaning on the front and characters on the back → nothing ticked, with "Anki can't check handwriting. Tick Writing only if you wrote the characters out…" (the owner: let the learner choose). Audio on the front, or a cloze → nothing.
- **Reading a field** (`cleanField`, `fieldText`): HTML and `&#…;` codes undone, then Unicode's composed form (`.normalize('NFC')`, as Anki does when it saves a note; a downloaded deck may store "a" and its accent apart). The lines of the deck's layout (headings such as "Common Meaning") are left out (`layoutLines`: a line in more than half a field's filled notes, of the first 200, at least 3, and somewhere beside other text, so a field that is just "noun" keeps it); the pop-up's example word reads fields the same way. A field with sentence marks (。？！「」：…) is kept whole, never cut at a bracket as "学校 (學校)" is.
- **Radicals** (the owner, 2026-10-21: "the most complete"): in a **deck of radicals** (`isRadicalDeck`: more than half of a note type's first 200 notes are on the ready-made radicals list, in either form), a note whose characters are a line of the list with no reading becomes that radical (`itemFromNote`, `radicalRecord`, `<traditional>|radical`, the list's meaning), with only its meaning and writing cards (`RADICAL_CARD_TYPES`); a radical that is a word (水) stays the word. Only in such a deck, because the list pairs full characters with side forms (言 with 讠): in a deck of characters, 言 stays the word yán. The list comes in `context.radicals` (the pop-up reads it with `dictionaryLoader.readyEntries('radicals')`). Radicals go into the Anki deck only, not the ready-made Radicals deck; the result counts them apart (`report.newRadicals`: "Brought over 106 new words and 108 radicals"), and the example shows "丨 (radical) · line (a stroke)". **Left as it is:** the Kangxi deck's Write template is guessed as Meaning (its front hides the radical in a drawing script); its Recognize notes usually have more reviews and win, and the learner can untick it.
- **Notes become words** through the dictionary (`wordFromNote`): the reading the note gives picks the dictionary entry (tone marks with or without spaces, numbers; `readsAs` in `src/dictionary.js`), which gives the other script's form and the Taiwan reading; a note without pinyin takes the usual reading (`likeliestEntry`). Then `checkWord`, as for a typed word.
- **A textbook's pinyin** (the owner, 2026-10-21, on Ch_book1's 對不起 and 剛剛 left out): a Taiwan textbook writes full tones where the dictionary has a neutral one (duìbùqǐ, gānggāng). Where no entry has the note's tones, an entry whose reading differs only by a neutral tone where the note has a full one is taken, with the dictionary's reading (對不起 duìbuqǐ; `readsAs(entry, typed, { fullForNeutral: true })` in `src/dictionary.js`); tone cards accept the full tone there anyway. The textbook's tones are not kept as a Taiwan reading (shown readings are never guessed, `docs/words.md`). Before reading, `tidyNotePinyin` leaves out notes in brackets ("Yèlǐ (li)", "xuéqī (tw: xuéqí)"), joins an erhua written apart ("Yīdiǎn er" → yīdiǎnr) and reads a breve as the third tone (Făguó). The reason for a note left out shows its pinyin as the note has it.
- **Which entry, and the forms of a word** (the owner, 2026-10-21, on the notes still left out): an entry read so in the **mainland comes before one read so only in Taiwan** (打 "dǎ" is dǎ "to hit", not dá "dozen", whose Taiwan reading is dǎ; this had made 14 of Hanzi 3000's characters the wrong word). A field with more than one form of the word (`charactersOf`) gives the first form the dictionary reads as the note's pinyin, of: the field without what is in brackets (照相（像）機 → 照相機, 著（着）急 → 著急), a single character then a word, the character written into the word (臺 / 台灣 → 臺灣), and each part; failing that, the first part, as before. **A slip:** pinyin that fits none of the entries of a word the dictionary has with one reading only takes that reading (咖啡 "kāifēi" → kāfēi; 一點兒 "Yīdiǎn er", 一会儿 "yī huì" → yīdiǎnr, yīhuìr with the r, so their tone cards come over too). **A word the dictionary lacks:** a run of syllables in its pinyin is split by the table of syllables, each mark in a syllable of its own (`splitByTones`: 公車站 "Gōngchē zhàn" → gōngchēzhàn), and its other script is made word by word, as a sentence's (公车站).
- **Sentences** (the owner, 2026-10-21): a note whose characters are a sentence or phrase (`looksLikeSentence`: a sentence's punctuation anywhere, or any punctuation in the part a word is read from) becomes one of the app's sentences (`sentenceFromNote`, `sentenceRecord` in `src/sentences.js`, ID `<traditional>|sentence`), not a word left out. In a **note type of sentences** (`isSentenceDeck`: more than half its notes with characters are sentences, like Ch_book1's grammar notes) every note is one, the phrases too (這個人, 我的書, 小錶 "a small watch"). A label before it is left out ("a: ", "a/b: "); the meaning is the note's, joined on one line, and a sentence without one is left out ("A sentence without its meaning"); the pinyin is the note's as written, for reading. The other script comes word by word (`sentenceForms`: `splitIntoWords`, then `otherScriptForm`; a word the dictionary can't decide stays as written), the sentence taken for simplified if some word is written so only in simplified. **Cards:** the reading card (`meaning`) takes the history of a template ticked for Meaning; no pinyin or tone cards. The **writing card** is chosen in the pop-up's **"Sentences · 88 notes"** box, shown when the file has any (`countSentenceNotes`): "Write the whole sentence" (`choices.sentenceWriting: 'whole'`, the default: Anki doesn't say which word was the point) or "No writing card" (`'none'`: it comes suspended, to be switched on in the sentence's panel); it takes a writing template's history otherwise. Sentences don't wait in Learn and get no `importedAt`. A note type of sentences says "Each note becomes a sentence, like: 我姓王。 · My name is Wang." and "Your Anki reviews count for their reading card." The result: "Brought over 491 new words and 85 sentences" (`report.newSentences`). **Tried on Ch_book1:** 85 sentences (its 87 grammar notes but 3 without a Chinese sentence, and 怎麼樣...?); their Anki history is on the grammar point's templates (Explanation, Structure), not on a sentence's, so they start new; 11 notes left out, from 149 at the start of the day.
- **Several readings in one note** (還 "Hái, huán", 看 "kān / kàn", 得 "děi, de"; the owner, 2026-10-21, over two words: Anki had one card): **one word with all of them**, pinyin "hai2, huan2" as the app keeps several readings (`docs/words.md`): its pinyin card shows them all, its tone card takes any, Anki's history goes over once. Each part (split at a comma or slash) is read as one reading is; a part without a mark is the neutral tone; kept are readings of Mandarin syllables, one for each character, and **only those the dictionary has, where it has any** (會 "huèi, huì" → huì; 像片 "xiàng piàn / xiàng piàn er" leaves out 像片兒's; 不 "Bù, bú" → bù). One reading left: an ordinary word. With the note's meaning empty, each reading's dictionary meaning, joined. The Taiwan reading is the entry's only for a single reading. A word with several readings is not the same word as one of them added later (還|hai2 is another ID).
- **Tried on Ch_book1:** every note with pinyin now comes over (491 words and 85 sentences, 2,301 reviews copied, against 430 words and 1,833 reviews that morning); the 3 left out are grammar notes without a Chinese sentence. HSK 2012: 3 more cards keep their schedule (erhua words with their r). Hanzi 3000: the same counts, its 14 wrong words right.
- **The meaning** (the owner, 2026-10-21: the deck's own definitions come before the dictionary's): the field chosen for it; where that is empty in a note, the note type's **spare meaning field** (`guessSpareMeaning`, kept as `spareMeaning` beside `roles`, not shown in the pop-up); only then the dictionary's (`proposeMeaning`, with the "Dictionary meanings" setting). The spare field is one not chosen for a role, **named** for a meaning (meaning, definition, english, gloss, 意思) and not for an example or sentence, with meanings in at least half the notes: a rule by content alone would take StrokeLabel ("1 stroke"), Japanese readings or the translations of examples. Its text goes through `meaningsIn`, then `proposeMeaning`: lines of characters, pinyin and zhuyin left out, "1." numbers, the measure words HSK 2012 glues on ("mountain rangemeasure word條/条tiao2"), CC-CEDICT's references cut as the app's dictionary has them (學習|学习[xue2 xi2] → 學習), and the same non-meanings the dictionary leaves out (`NOT_A_MEANING`, as in `scripts/build-dictionary.js`: "variant of…", "also written…", "Taiwan pr."). A field with an entry for each reading (鱼: "Yú … surname Yu", then "yú … fish") gives the lines under the word's reading: the dictionary entry's, with a capital only if the entry has one, since HSK 2012 writes 冷 "Lěng" for lěng "cold". **Tried:** HSK 2012 takes "Definitions" for 577 of its 578 notes with an empty SimpleMeaning (打篮球 has none); 25 differ from the app's dictionary, by the deck's older edition (年 "year; grain; harvest (old)"). **Not done:** Ch_book1's 16 words with their meaning only in "Part of Speech" (瓶 "(unclassified) bound form), measure for bottles of") keep the dictionary's, since that field holds "adverb, verb" in the other notes; the owner, 2026-10-21: leave them so. Notes that fail are listed afterwards with the reason, as the list import does.
- **Words already there** (the owner, 2026-10-21): a word the learner has keeps its meaning and everything else; it is put in the decks (`report.wordsThere`). Its pinyin is the note's already, being part of its ID. Where the note has a meaning of its own (`fromDeck` from `wordFromNote`: the meaning field or the spare one, not the dictionary's; a sentence's always) and it differs from the word's (`report.otherMeanings`), the check step shows a box **"Words you already have"** with the checkbox **"Use the deck's meanings for the 194 words you already have"**, off by default (`choices.useDeckMeanings`), and the hint "Otherwise they keep the meaning they have. A meaning you wrote yourself is replaced too." (the owner's choice: the app doesn't record which meanings were edited). The count (`countOtherMeanings`) plans only the notes whose characters hold a form of a word already there, and no cards (`onlyCharacters` in the context): 0.2 s against 1.4 s for the whole plan on Mei's file, the same count. With the Sentences box it is made again when a deck is ticked or a field chosen (`showMoreChoices` in `src/ankiImportForm.js`). The result says "194 words you already had were given the deck's meaning." The new meaning is saved through `putAll`, so `changedAt` is stamped and combining backups takes it. **Tried:** Ch_book1, then Mei's HSK deck: 271 words already there, 194 with another meaning.
- **The result** (`showResult`; the owner, 2026-10-21: one paragraph was cramped, and what became of the notes left out unclear): a line each for the new words (and radicals), words already there added to the decks, cards that keep their schedule with how many reviews, words waiting in Learn, cards left alone, notes that were the same word as another; numbers with commas (4,862). Then **"9 notes were left out"** (`anki-left-out`), "They weren't brought over.", and the notes grouped by reason, each reason once with its notes on a line under it and what to do about them under those (`adviceFor`; the owner, 2026-10-21, as "One word" was the advice for sentences too): for a word, "To study one, add it yourself: open a deck, tap “+ Add to deck”, then “One word”."; for a sentence without its meaning, the same with "“A sentence”, with its meaning"; for a sentence without Chinese (Ch_book1's grammar notes), "They explain rather than give something to write, and the app has no card for that." (a grammar explanation would be a new kind of card, the owner). The reasons are said about the note (in `wordFromNote`), not as `checkWord`'s advice for typing: "No meaning: the note has none, and the app's dictionary doesn't have this word" (or "has this word only with another reading"), "No pinyin: …", "The note's pinyin “…” can't be read."

## Schedules

- **Exact due dates** (the owner, 2026-10-19). For each card brought over, every review in Anki's log is copied as a review here (`fromAnki: true`, `grade` = Anki's button, `duration` = how long the answer took in ms), and one more record holds the schedule Anki gave the card: `importedSchedule: { fsrsState, due }` in place of a grade, dated a millisecond after its last Anki review. `replay` in `src/scheduler.js` goes on from that record, so the card is due when Anki said and the app's FSRS takes over at its next review, while rebuilding from reviews and combining backups still give the same card. The card record takes that schedule as it is, without replaying (`planAnkiImport`).
- **Speed:** `replay` works out only the grade given (ts-fsrs `next`) and turns the card into plain data once at the end (2026-10-21; checked to give exactly what `gradeOptions` gives, review after review, on the 56,048 copied reviews of Mei's file and in `tests/logic.test.js`). Planning Mei's file (3,996 cards) takes about 1 s per script in Node, from 9 to 10 s.
- **The FSRS state** in that record: the one the copied reviews give, with Anki's due date, interval, `reps`, `lapses` and state; stability and difficulty are Anki's own where Anki used FSRS (`{"s":…,"d":…}` in the card's `data`), otherwise those worked out from the reviews (as Anki does when someone switches to FSRS). A card without a log gets the interval as stability and difficulty 5.
- **Which reviews count:** those after the last "Forget" (a manual log entry with no interval), without manual changes of due date or anything ungraded. A card new in Anki brings nothing; a word whose cards are all new waits in Learn, in Anki's order of new cards.
- **One source per card** (the owner): where two templates feed one kind, or two notes are the same word, the Anki card with the most reviews counts; `report.sameWord` counts such notes.
- **Tone is ticked with Pinyin** (the owner, 2026-10-21: reviewing pinyin is how most people practice tones in Anki): wherever `guessCardTypes` ticks Pinyin it ticks Tone too, so the tone card gets the same Anki card's reviews and schedule. A box left unticked starts new; until 2026-10-21 a hidden rule copied the pinyin card to an unticked tone card, and the box read wrongly. A tone card comes only for a word that can have one (a syllable per character).
- **Suspended in Anki** (queue −1) stays suspended here (the word's `suspended`).
- **Cards without history** (usually writing, and any kind no template was ticked for) come in Review a few a day: a word with cards from Anki has `importedAt`, and its missing cards join the queue of cards learned again after a change of script (`toLearnAgain` in `planSession`), limited by new words per day, with the lesson; the counts say "· 3 more new today" (`fromAnki`).
- **Importing again** changes nothing studied: a card already here is left as it is (`report.alreadyStudied`), and copied reviews are named after Anki's (`anki-<Anki's review ID>-<type>`, the schedule `anki-<Anki's card ID>-<type>-schedule`), the one exception to random review IDs, so the same file imported on two devices gives the same reviews when combined.

## Names in the code

- `src/ankiFile.js`: `filesInZip`, `unpack` (the browser's `DecompressionStream`), `openAnkiFile(bytes, SQL)`, `readCollection(db)` (both schemas into one shape). Anki's tables are kept in its own text order, "unicase", which sql.js can't be taught; the copy in memory is told it is SQLite's NOCASE and opened again.
- `src/ankiImport.js` (plain calculation, `tests/ankiImport.test.js`): `cleanField`, `layoutLines`, `fieldText`, `fieldsInTemplate`, `isPinyin`, `guessRoles`, `guessCardTypes`, `describeCollection`, `defaultChoices`, `isRadicalDeck`, `itemFromNote`, `wordFromNote`, `planAnkiImport` → `{ words, decks, reviews, cards, report }`.
- `src/ankiImportForm.js`: the pop-up; `saveImport` in `src/db.js` saves it all in one transaction.
- **Libraries** (the owner's approval, 2026-10-19): `sql.js` (SQLite compiled to WebAssembly, MIT; fetched only when a file is read: a 40 KB script and a 685 KB `.wasm`, kept offline across versions with the dictionary, `scripts/build-offline.js`) and `fzstd` (zstd, MIT, 24 KB). Credits in `credits-dialog`.
- **Tests:** `tests/fixtures/anki/` holds a small collection made by Anki itself in four exports, with the script that makes them (`make-fixtures.py`, needs `pip install anki`); `tests/browser/anki.browser.js` imports in the real app. For trying it by hand on real decks: four shared decks from GitHub in the git-ignored `local/anki-decks/downloads/`, and `scripts/anki-study-data.py`, which gives them the made-up histories of three learners in Anki (SM-2 and FSRS, leeches, Forget, Set due date, a filtered deck, a lapsed backlog, an export in the middle of a session) and exports them in every format to `local/anki-decks/study-data/` (`local/README.md`).

## Not built

- **Keeping the page usable during a long import** (the owner, 2026-10-21: a consideration, left for now; to look at again after trying the import on a phone). At about a phone's speed Mei's HSK deck holds the page about 5 s to read and 13 s to import; the ring (item 5 above) shows it is working, but nothing can be tapped meanwhile. Two ways: **planning in batches** (`planAnkiImport` async, a pause for the page every few hundred notes): responsive, and a count like "1,200 of 4,995 notes" becomes possible; no new file, but it doesn't help the reading (sql.js opening the file is one block; how the 5 s divide is not measured). Or a **Web Worker** (Vite builds it, no dependency; `scripts/build-offline.js` takes it from `dist/assets/`): also cancellable, but the worker needs its own copy of the 8.4 MB dictionary (memory and seconds on a phone) unless the dictionary moves there for the whole app, `likeliestEntry` and `randomId` can't be passed to it, and a few hundred lines more. Batches first, the worker if the reading is the slow part.
- **A button for each reason a note was left out** (the owner, 2026-10-21: a to-do), opening the right add form ("One word" or "A sentence") in a deck, in place of the advice saying where it is.

- Anki's own scheduling settings (retention, FSRS parameters): the app's defaults take over after the first review.
- An export to Anki (`docs/history.md`, "Nice to have").
- A very large collection is read whole into memory; with media in it, exporting without media keeps it small.
