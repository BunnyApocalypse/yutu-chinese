# Things that are easy to get wrong

## The study area

- **The app says "Loading…" until the first screen is ready** (`main.starting` in `index.html`, removed when `startUp` in `src/main.js` ends; 2026-10-26: the owner saw an empty writing area while the app started). The review header and study area are kept in place but `visibility: hidden`, so nothing is measured differently. **Nothing slow may come before the first card:** Review reads the reviews afterwards (`docs/cards.md`, "Streaks"). Tested in `tests/browser/startup.browser.js`, which holds reads back by keeping a writing transaction open.
- **The study area is shared by all card kinds.** `showPartsFor` in `src/review.js` decides which parts show for each kind, and Free practice needs the writing parts back (`deactivate`). A new element in the study area must be added there.
- **A flow that is not on screen must ignore taps.** `toneFlow` and `recallCard` both listen on the card face; each checks it has a current card, and `review.js` calls `stop()` on both before the next card.
- **On a tone card a tap is an answer** (a dot is the neutral tone). A mark begins only on a finger coming down (`pointerdown`), so the touch that brought a character up is never taken for a dot; no pause is needed (until 2026-10-24, 300 ms). A stroke the recognizer can't classify is neither answer nor mistake; the card says so and waits. The tone mark's layer is removed by `replaceChildren`; `ask()` puts it back each time.
- **The writing area is an SVG element, which has no `.hidden` property.** Hide its row (`#writing-row`) or use `toggleAttribute('hidden', ...)`.
- **In Review the outline is switched off at the start of every word**; left on, it counts as peeking and every suggestion becomes Again. **`wordFlow.start` sets the outline before the stroke data loads**, so "Hide outline" tapped while loading isn't undone.
- **A dialog's `close` event can come late**, after the next tap is handled: closing a word's page once closed the popup of a word tapped on the card beneath, so the next tap moved a lesson on. A `close` handler must check that what it undoes belongs to its dialog (`dialog.contains(peekCard)` in `src/wordPage.js`). It can even come after the same dialog was opened again: the streak screen's ignores it while open (2026-10-24; it opened the menu over the screen once the streak was counted quickly enough).
- **Fold-out headings (`summary`) can't be selected**: with a pen, a tap that slides would select the text, and a tablet then neither opens nor closes the fold.

## The writing area's size

- **The writing area is the point of the app and must stay the biggest thing on the screen** (the owner, 2026-10-14, after it was made smaller to fit a lesson's rows; that was undone). **The rule, in the owner's words: the top and the bottom are sized to hold everything that can appear in Learn and in Review, and the canvas stays a constant size.** `chooseSize` in `src/wordFlow.js` reserves a fixed height for everything else (`ROOM_ABOVE_BELOW`, 305 since 2026-10-27; 40 more with OpenDyslexic, `DYSLEXIC_ROOM`; and 30 more for each time 400px the area is bigger than a phone's, since the meaning grows with it, `ZOOMED_ROOM`). Room is found by trimming the rows around it, never by raising that number. The four grade buttons must fit side by side at phone width, and the review screen fit without scrolling. **Until 2026-10-27 it was 380**, and a 360×640 phone's area was 260px where its width allows 328 (the owner: "if the canvas isn't optimally large on small phones then thats a problem"); now 328, 343 on a 375×667, 696 on a 768×1024 tablet (was 644); a 390×844 phone is limited by its width as before. `tests/browser/areaSize.browser.js` checks 360×640. A 320×568 phone (the first iPhone SE) still scrolls a little, as it did before: its tools and the lesson's notes wrap. So:
  - **one row of buttons under the area, never two** (2026-10-27): the tools, the grades, a lesson's "Next step", the tone buttons, or the verdicts between characters, each in the others' place. "Practice again" lies on the area's bottom right edge (`.practice-again`), as "About this word" on its left; while practicing a word already answered, the tools take the grades' place and the link reads "Done practicing", bringing the grades back (`gradesAway`, `donePracticing` in `src/review.js`), as does writing the character; in a lesson's tone step "Next step" takes the tone buttons' place once answered;
  - **the line saying a word is in another script lies over the area** (`#script-flag`, `.study-area > .script-flag`: no height, its text in the top left corner of the writing area or the card's face, which gets room for it on a face, `.card-face.flagged`); in two columns it is a line of the side column;
  - every line above the area keeps its room when empty (meaning, pinyin, the slots and status line), and nothing is reserved under the buttons; **nothing is added under the grades, and nothing above the area changes height while a word is written** (the owner, from a screencast). **With OpenDyslexic** the status line is under the slots, a size smaller, in a row of fixed height (it wrapped beside two or three slots, and the area moved while writing; 2026-10-27), and Clear, Show outline and Animate are a size smaller so they keep to one row; changing the font rebuilds the area (`sizeForFont` in `src/main.js`);
  - **focus:** while a card is up, the screen's name and the counts line are put away (`main.focused`, `setFocus`) and ☰ (or the way back) sits in the corner;
  - **the meaning has a fixed room for the screen** (`src/meaningFit.js`; the owner, 2026-10-25, after more than half the HSK meanings were cut on a phone): one line at the smaller size's height, plus up to 45px where the screen has height to spare (`MEANING_EXTRA_MAX`, `--meaning-extra`, taken from the push-down in `buildWritingArea`), so up to three lines. In it: the meaning bold at `--size-big` on as many lines as fit, else at `--size-title` (no smaller: the owner, "within limits"), else whole meanings left out from the end (`.sense`, `.cut-after`: "good; proper; …"). **What it doesn't use goes above it** (`--meaning-unused`, added to the margin under the kind of card), so the area is in the same place for every meaning; while a card is up the line has no min-height of its own. A tap on a cut one opens it all in a panel (`clampWithPopup` in `src/clamp.js`, which calls `fitMeaning` first and is called again after a turn of the screen or a font arriving). **On a big area the meaning grows with it** (`--meaning-zoom`, the area's size over 400 as for the card's face, and its extra room by as much, unrounded; the owner, 2026-10-25, from pictures of the tablet), and the line is as wide as the area rather than the header's 520px; not in two columns. Whether it fits is counted in lines with no limit set (`linesNeeded`), never by comparing `scrollHeight` with `clientHeight`, which the browser rounds apart by a pixel or two where lines are a fraction of a pixel tall; what is left over is measured with `getBoundingClientRect`, or the card moved by a pixel. A small phone (375×667) has no height to spare: one line. A sentence's writing card keeps its own layout (two lines) and is not fitted. Tested in `tests/browser/meaning.browser.js`;
  - **a word's reading line above the area keeps to one line** (`showPinyin` in `src/review.js`, `#prompt-pinyin.one-line`; 2026-10-26, when zhuyin and pinyin together with the other region's reading took three lines on a 360px phone): if it is too long, the other region's reading is left out, then the reading is a size smaller inside the line (a `span`, so the line keeps its height), then cut with "…". A sentence's still wraps;
  - **the slots and the status line share one row** (`.progress-row`); a word of one character has no slots (`.word-row.single`) but the row keeps its height. Only a word too long for the width wraps it;
  - **a lesson's note must fit beside "I know this word" on one line on a phone**, or the area moves down at that step;
  - **the card's top line** (kind of card, the deck studied in Review, About and Edit, the loudspeaker) keeps 30px clear of the corner under 600px wide, with tighter gaps under 390px (`main.focused .type-row`). It **sits on the corner's center line** (main's top padding 6px while a card is up; the owner, 2026-10-18). **In Learn the kind of card is centered** (`main.learning`), at the left in Review; **in Learn today's words take its place** (`#learn-strip`, 28px boxes in the 36px row, so the row keeps its height; its middle column never wider than the room beside the corner's buttons, so it can tell when the words don't fit, `docs/cards.md`). It is `--size-small`, except in Review under 390px wide, where it would take two lines. **The deck's name (`deck-tag`) is the one thing on it that shrinks**: the rest keep their width (`flex-shrink: 0`; "Show pinyin" took two lines at 360px).
- **Its largest size is 760px, in one column or two** (`AREA_MAX` in `src/wordFlow.js`; the owner, 2026-10-25: at 640 a third of an upright tablet was empty). **On a screen taller than the card needs, the card is moved down by a third of the room left over** (`--card-offset`, set with the size in `buildWritingArea`, as the margin under `main.focused .type-row`; half until 2026-10-26, when the owner found the box a little low on the tablet), after the meaning's room is taken, so it never costs the meaning anything: the kind of card stays in the corner and nothing moves within a card. Centering by the style sheet was tried and not kept: the area moved 29px as the grades came up. A 375×667 phone has no room to spare; two columns get none.
- **The card sits at the same height whatever its kind** (the owner: the canvas jumped between a lesson's steps): `showPartsFor` makes the slots and status line invisible (`ghost`) rather than removing them, and the card's face is exactly the writing area's size. **A new word's first look** fills the meaning and reading above the card with the word's own and makes them `ghost` too (`showLessonStep`), so they take the room they take at step 2 (until 2026-10-31 the card jumped down at step 2: 37px on a 360×640 phone, 75px on a 390×844); the pill saying a word was learned floats over them (`.learned-pill-place`). `local/learn-check/measure.js` (git-ignored) measured it. With a tip on it, the face scrolls and "About this word" stays at its bottom edge (`position: sticky`); when the tip fits, word and tip are centered above it. **A tone card's face looks like the writing area**, its guide lines a picture used as a stencil (`mask-image`) over `--grid`, since a named color can't be used inside a picture.
- **Two columns on a screen held sideways** (2026-10-17; the owner: "we wont be able to stop people from going into landscape"): where clearly bigger (`chooseSize`: wider than tall, at least 60px gained), the area or card face is placed on the right (☰ stays clear, buttons away from a right-hand pen), up to 760px, the rest in a 360px column on its left (`data-layout="wide"`, `WIDE_SIDE_COLUMN`, `--wide-side`). **Not to do again:** as a grid, text widening the second column shifted the area under the pen; and a column taller than the screen made the atlas's full-page picture resize the window, which rebuilt the area and wiped the character. The atlas has `tablet-wide` and `phone-wide`. **The grades on a tablet held sideways** (the owner, 2026-10-20, from pictures, after tester 3 found them small): stacked one a line across the 360px column (`--tap-large`), Again at the top, at the column's bottom with their bottom level with the card's (main as tall as the area, the study area filling it, the grades `order: 10` and `margin-top: auto`, the empty footer taking no room). Only from 640px tall (`@media (min-height: 640px)`): on a phone held sideways the stack made the column taller than the screen, which scrolled the page and wiped the character in the atlas; there the four stay side by side.
- **A card's text grows with it** (the owner: "comically small" on a big screen): `.card-face` is zoomed by `--face-zoom` (area size over 400, at least 1). **`zoom` also scales placement and what is measured on it**: `top`/`right` are divided by the zoom in two columns, and so are the tone mark's points (`zoomOf` in `src/toneFlow.js`).
- The window `resize` handler reacts only to width changes: on Android the keyboard changes the height, and that must not wipe a character in progress.

## Hanzi Writer

- **It remembers quiz options from one quiz to the next.** Pass `quizStartStrokeNum` on every `quiz()` call (0 for a normal start), or a later quiz silently starts partway through.
- **Don't replace its character from inside one of its own callbacks.** `resume` in `src/quiz.js` waits a tick first.
- **`charData` in `src/quiz.js` is what Hanzi Writer holds, which may be rearranged; `baseData` is the data as loaded.** Its stroke numbers refer to `charData`; `altOrder` and `sequence` to `baseData`.
- **Never create a new Hanzi Writer per character**: each adds touch listeners that are never removed. Use `setCharacter()` (the one exception: rebuilding after a screen rotation).
- **The writing area is emptied before a new word or character loads** (`blank()` in `src/quiz.js`, from `wordFlow.start` and `showCharacter`; the owner, 2026-10-24, saw the character before flash up): your strokes, the printed character and the outline go at once, and the outline is held back until `start()`, or it would be the old character's. Tested by holding the stroke data back (`review.browser.js`).
- **`setCharacter()` rebuilds its layers and hides the outline**, and it adds its layers only once the data has loaded, later ones on top; so `start()` re-appends the layer of drawn strokes and re-applies the outline setting each time.
- **It colors a radical's strokes separately** (`radicalColor`, defaulting to `strokeColor` only at creation): change both (`setPrintedColor`).
- **It works out touch positions from the SVG's on-screen size**, so the `width`/`height` passed to it must match the real size. Don't scale the SVG with CSS. The area needs `touch-action: none`, or drawing scrolls the page on a phone. A stroke begun just outside it, going down, could still pull the page down and reload it (the owner, 2026-10-24): while a card is up, `html:has(main.focused)` has `overscroll-behavior-y: contain`, which switches the browser's pull-to-refresh off.

## Stroke data

- **Only 1,013 traditional characters have Taiwan-standard stroke data.** The big set (`hanzi-writer-data`, from Make Me a Hanzi) draws traditional characters in mainland forms: 為 as 爲 (12 strokes), 說 as 説, 辶 and 艹 with 3 strokes. animCJK fixes those it covers; the rest (吃, 真, 骨) may differ from the Taiwan standard. Taiwan's MOE data would be complete but is CC BY-NC-ND, so it can't be converted and redistributed.
- **The script decides the form even for characters both scripts share** (花, 起, 道), and **stroke numbers can differ between the sets** (花: 8 in one, 7 in the other). Always pass the script to `loadCharData`; never cache by character alone; derive stroke numbers from the data in use, as `src/shortcuts.js` does.
- The slot row sets `lang` so the device picks the right regional font, not Japanese or mainland glyphs.
- Vite's dev server answers a missing file with the app's HTML page and status 200, so `loadCharData` treats "not valid JSON" as "no stroke data".
- The copy script ships `ARPHICPL.TXT` alongside the stroke data.

## The browser and the network

- `crypto.randomUUID()` only exists on HTTPS or localhost, so it fails on the LAN dev address; `randomId()` in `src/db.js` uses `crypto.getRandomValues()`.
- Service workers and installing only work over HTTPS or on `localhost`, so they can't be tested from a phone on the LAN dev address: use the deployed test copy, or the built app at a `localhost` address on the computer (`docs/offline.md`).
