# Cards, scheduling, learning and reviewing

## Cards and scheduling

- **Four card types per word, as in Skritter**, each with its own schedule (`cards` store, keyed `<wordId>#<type>`), introduced one at a time in the order meaning, pinyin, tone, writing, **each word starting at a different kind** (`startingTypeFirst` in `src/session.js`; the owner, 2026-10-20, after tester 3 saw only meaning cards in her first Review): the start goes round the kinds the word can have by its `order` (0 meaning, 1 pinyin, 2 tone, 3 writing, 4 meaning…) and carries on from the end to the start, so the first Review after learning a few words mixes kinds. A writing card first is fine: Learn has just taught it, and it comes without the lesson (`taughtInLearn`).
  - `meaning`: see the word, recall the meaning; reveal and self-grade.
  - `pinyin`: see the word and its meaning above it, recall the reading; reveal and self-grade.
  - `tone`: see the word and its meaning above it, give each character's tone with five buttons or by drawing the mark (details below). A word whose pinyin doesn't have one syllable per character gets no tone card.
- **Words with several readings** (好 hǎo and hào are two words; the owner, 2026-10-24: 好 answered hǎo counted wrong for hào; the owner chose this on 2026-10-25): tone and pinyin cards always show the meaning in the line above the card, Chinese left out as on a writing card (`showAskedMeaning` in `src/review.js`; CC-CEDICT writes readings in meanings, "variant of 好[hao3]"). A meaning card shows the reading under the word only when another of the learner's words is written with the same characters (`hasSameCharacters`, `showReading` in `src/recallCard.js`), since otherwise it helps recall the meaning. Tested in `tests/browser/readings.browser.js`. Once a tone or pinyin card is answered, the line above is emptied, the meaning being in the answer on the card (`offerGrades`; the owner, 2026-10-25).
  - `writing`: see the meaning, write the word.
- **The prompt stands out and sits by the writing area** (tester 4, 2026-10-21: up under the kind of card it read as a heading, and they began writing the wrong character; the owner chose B of two, 2026-10-22; pictures `local/tester4/prompt-*.png`): `#prompt-meaning` bold at `--size-big`, below the pinyin's line (`order` in `.review-header`), so the pinyin, once shown, is above it. Its line keeps the old height (`calc(1.5 * var(--size-title))`, line-height 1.25), so nothing moved; a sentence's meaning keeps the old size, bold.
- **Writing prompts show the meaning only** (the owner): no pinyin. "Show pinyin" reveals it for near-synonyms without affecting the grade; it is shown once the word is finished. Glosses must therefore be distinguishable from each other. **Chinese in the meaning is left out there** (`meaningWithoutChinese` in `src/dictionary.js`; tester 4 wrote 您 for 你, whose meaning names it; the owner, 2026-10-22): brackets holding only Chinese go ("(CL:把)"), other Chinese becomes **a small gray box** (`.chinese-gap`, `fillWithoutChinese` in `src/review.js`; the owner chose it from pictures over "…" and "[字]", 2026-10-24: "…" was unclear, and CC-CEDICT itself writes "..." for words left out). The box holds "…" out of sight for the panel of a cut-off meaning, and is named "Chinese left out" for a screen reader. The stored meaning is unchanged; other cards show it whole. **Except a radical's full form** (the owner, 2026-10-26: "the left-radical form of Kangxi radical 145, □" didn't say which radical): Chinese right after "side, top, bottom or left-radical form of" or "Kangxi radical N" stays (`FULL_FORM_BEFORE`), marked with `lang`; not every radical's Chinese, since 匚's "occurring in 区, 医, 匹" shows its shape, nor "erhua form of …" and the like.
- **Cards of one word are kept apart** (`SIBLING_GAP_MS`, 3 minutes), since each gives away the others' answers. With few words in play the app says "Next card in N minutes" although cards exist: intended, and **said why** (the owner, 2026-10-20): when the next card is held back only by this gap (`nextHeldBack` from `planSession`), the end of Review adds "Cards of the same word are kept a few minutes apart, so one doesn't give away another's answer." (`.done-why`).
- **FSRS** with default parameters (90% retention, learning steps of 1 and 10 minutes, fuzz on). A setting for target retention is on the roadmap.
- **A Review of a set number of cards** (tester 4, 2026-10-21; the owner chose a start screen with a setting to switch it off, and "Keep going" at the end, 2026-10-22; pictures `local/tester4/review-set-number.png`): opening Review with more than 20 cards to do (due and new; `SMALLEST_LIMIT`, the smallest number offered) shows `#review-start`: "243 cards to review", "Review all" (the primary), 20, 50, 100 (only those below the total) and "Other…" (a number box). The choice is the sitting (`sitting` in `src/review.js`: `limit`, null for all, and `done`, counted in `grade`; corrections looked back at don't count). With a number, the kind of card says "Writing · 3/100" (not the counts line, which is put away while a card is up), and at the number Review shows "100 cards done", "143 cards still to review.", the streak line and "Keep going: 100 more" (`review-more-button`), not "Learn new words". The sitting lasts until Review is left: `activate` asks again. "Ask how many cards to review" (`reviewStart`, on by default, Settings › Daily study) off starts on all at once, as before; the browser tests switch it off in the harness, as they do tips.
- **Session order:** due cards first (longest overdue first), then new cards of words already introduced; the day starts at 4:00.
- **Settings** (`settings` store): `script`, `pronunciation`, `rawInput`, `newWordsPerDay`, `enabledTypes`, `leechLapses`, `leechAction`, `streaks`, `streakCards`, `streakWords`, `sound`, `sentenceReading`, `sentenceWriting`, and more. A type switched off never appears but keeps its cards and history; at least one stays on.
- **Reviews** store the final verdicts (`passedPerChar`), the proposed ones (`suggestedPerChar`), and `mistakesPerChar`. Dates are numbers (milliseconds); FSRS state is plain JSON with ISO dates, so records go into a backup unchanged.
- **No sync.** Each browser on each device has its own data; Export/Import backup moves it as one JSON file, combined or replacing (`docs/merging.md`).
- **Free practice records nothing** (`startFreePractice` in `src/main.js`, `practice` in `src/review.js`): writing works for any typed characters; the other kinds need the word in the word list. No grade buttons (`docs/interface.md`, "Practice").

## The tone card

- **The buttons show bare tone marks** (ˉ ˊ ˇ ˋ ·; the owner, 2026-10-15): the word's own syllable would give its reading away. Checked automatically; a right answer moves on by itself, a wrong one waits for a tap; one wrong tone suggests Again. **A right answer's pause holds nobody up** (the owner, 2026-10-26, choosing "B" of four ideas: the 500 ms showing the green syllable, `MOVE_ON_AFTER_MS`, felt like lag): a tone button or a mark drawn meanwhile answers the next character, and a tap on the card goes on at once (after the last character, to the result); a dot begun in the pause is that tap, not the neutral tone (`hurried` in `src/toneFlow.js`). Not chosen: no pause at all, a shorter one, or the whole word on one face with each syllable filled in where it is answered.
- **The spoken tone of 一 and 不 is right too** (the owner, 2026-10-25, on 一些 "yì xiē"): 一 as yí before a fourth tone and yì before others (not as a number, `spokenSyllables` in `src/speechPlan.js`), 不 as bú before a fourth, since many textbooks write these (`spokenReadingsOf` in `src/tones.js`). A third tone before a third (ní hǎo) is not accepted: nobody writes it.
- **While asking** (the owner, 2026-10-16, after a tester took the big characters for something to draw over): the row above shows the word's characters in boxes with "Tone of 你 (1 of 2)" ("Which tone?" for one); inside the card the character small (`.tone-char`); under it **the five marks drawn faintly as examples** (level, rising, down then up, falling, the neutral dot; `.tone-examples`, `exampleMarks` in `src/toneFlow.js`; the owner, 2026-10-20, choosing draft B of three from pictures after tester 3 didn't know what to draw; pictures, since a font puts tone marks high and small; hidden from screen readers, the buttons naming each tone); under them, large, "Draw one of these here" and "or use the buttons below".
- **"What are tones?"** (the owner, 2026-10-21): a quiet link on the card's face under "or use the buttons below", while asking only, opening "Basics of Chinese" at its screen on tones (`docs/interface.md`). **Only until 3 tone cards have been graded** (`TONE_HELP_UNTIL`, `offerToneHelp` in `src/review.js`, passed as `offerHelp`; the owner, 2026-10-21: by then the learner shows they understand). A lesson's tone step is practice and not saved, and "I know this word" (`markedKnown`) answers nothing, so neither counts; reviews brought over from Anki do. On the face because the card's top line has no room in Learn (it took two lines beside "New word · step 2 of 5"); its touches are not taken for a mark, as with the arrows.
- **Right and wrong changed by a tap on a character's box**, exactly as on a writing card (the owner, 2026-10-24): once a character's tone is answered its box is a button (`setVerdict` in `src/toneFlow.js`), the tone given staying as it was; once all are answered the suggestion follows (`onVerdictChange`, `toneSuggestion` in `src/review.js`: Again if any is wrong, otherwise Good), and a wrong tone counted as right still says "You answered …". Also with one character, whose box shows on a tone card. On a card looked back at, each character is a square, green or red, a tap changing its verdict and the grade (`showPastCard` in `src/pastCard.js`, `changeVerdict`), with the meaning and reading above as on a writing card.
- **The arrows are on a tone card too** (the owner, 2026-10-17; `src/toneFlow.js`): › skips a character, counted as right (a dashed box); ‹ goes back, and one already answered is asked again as practice, its first answer standing. Only on a question.
- **A tap that moves on must be a new touch** (`freshTaps` in `src/freshTap.js`): after a character is written, an answer shown or given, or a card looked back at comes up, only a finger that came down afterwards counts, however soon. So the touch that finished a step never also moves on, and nothing has to wait (the owner, 2026-10-24: the 200 ms pause that did this before made the card feel unresponsive).

## Grading a written word

- **Each character gets a verdict, and one missed character fails the word.** Its box turns green or red; tapping the box overrules it. `judgeChar` (`src/session.js`) proposes "missed" if a hint flashed, the outline or animation was used, a finished character was restarted, or mistakes exceed a quarter of its strokes (one slip is always forgiven). **In Review, Show outline lasts one character**: moving to the next switches it off (`goTo` in `src/wordFlow.js`, for flows started with `hideOutline`; tester 4, 2026-10-21, saw it carry on to the second character, which then counted as peeked). A word of one character shows no boxes: its grade is its verdict (`grade` in `src/review.js`).
- **"✕ 姓 missed" and "✓ 姓 right" between characters** (the owner, 2026-10-21: in 我姓王, 姓 written right but judged missed, and the box went unnoticed): in a word or sentence of several characters in Review, once a character but not the last is written, two buttons in the grades' place (`#verdict-row`, `renderVerdictButtons` in `src/wordFlow.js`, option `verdictButtons`), the app's verdict pressed (`aria-pressed`, the box's colors, bold). A tap changes the verdict, as a tap on the box does; Next or a tap on the writing area moves on. Meanwhile Clear, Show outline and Animate are put away (`tools-away`, as under the grades), or a 360 by 640 phone scrolled; a swipe up still writes the character again. After the last character the grades take the place: its box, or the grade, overrules it. Not in Learn or Free practice.
- **`suggestGrade`:** Again if any character is missed, Hard if any mistakes, otherwise Good; never Easy. Any of the four can be picked; tapping the writing area takes the suggestion. Overruling a verdict updates it live. **Keys 1 to 4** press Again to Easy (`gradeByKey`; the owner, 2026-10-15) while the grades are up, no pop-up is open and nothing is being typed.
- **Two grade buttons, Wrong and Right** (setting "Grade buttons" under Daily study, `gradeButtons`: `'four'`, the default for everyone, or `'two'`; the owner, 2026-10-20, after tester 3, who had no Chinese): Wrong records Again, **Right always records Good**, whatever was suggested (`gradeFor` and `gradeButtons` in `src/scheduler.js`); Hard and Easy are never used, which FSRS handles well. A suggested Hard or Good highlights Right, and a tap on the writing area takes it as Good. Keys 1 and 2. The suggestion is saved as it was (`suggestedGrade` 2 for Hard). Two buttons share the row on a phone (`.grade-row.two`), and are stacked on a tablet held sideways like the four. A setting of the learner, so combining backups brings it to their other devices.
- **Once a card is answered, only the grades are under it** (the owner, 2026-10-17): `offerGrades` puts away Clear, Show outline, Animate and the tone buttons; `showPartsFor` and `onCharStart` bring back what's needed. **In a lesson's writing steps** (2026-10-18), once written only "Next step" or "Done" stays, with "Practice again" (`putToolsAway`, `tools-away` on `#writing-buttons`); writing again brings the tools back.
- **Practicing a written word again** (the owner, 2026-10-17; must never change the grading): "Practice again" (`practice-again-button`) or a swipe up writes the character on screen again, as often as wanted. Nothing counts (`practicing` in `src/wordFlow.js`, option `practiceWhenDone`); the status says "Practice", then "Practice · 1 mistake" (kept short so it doesn't wrap on a small phone); a tap doesn't grade; the arrows do nothing. **A character gone back to while the word is unfinished** (‹) is practiced too, and there a tap on the area goes on to the one still to be written, as Next does (the owner, 2026-10-21: only the arrow worked). **The current box keeps a blue ring** around its green or red (`.slot.current.passed`, `.missed`; the owner, 2026-10-21: after writing a character again, which one was on screen could not be told). Clear, Show outline and Animate come back **under** the grades, which don't move (`practicing` on the study area). A swipe once a character is done is taken by `src/quiz.js` (`canSwipeWhenDone`) and stopped there, so it isn't also the tap that moves on.
- **Skip and back arrows** (the owner, 2026-10-17): over the writing area's edges on a phone, where width limits the area (358px instead of 294), and beside it where the margins have room (`arrowsBeside`, `--arrow-room`, `arrows-beside`). Skip (›) in a word of several characters moves on and counts the character passed (in 為什麼, practice only 為); mistakes before still count; skipped characters are saved as `skippedPerChar` and shown dashed. **Back (‹) always goes to the character before**: a written one is practiced, changing nothing; a skipped one is written after all; the unfinished one goes on with its attempt, keeping its mistakes and hint (`goTo`, `hintShownNow`). **Characters are not always finished in order**: `wordFlow` tracks `finishedPerChar`, the word is done when all are (`wordFinished`), Next goes to `nextUnfinished`. Never test for "the last character" by position.

- **Going back to cards already graded** (the owner, 2026-10-21: "the last 10 or so cards I've studied should be able to be kept and I should be able to go back and change my grading"; the raw input kept, the owner's choice). **‹ on a card not yet touched** (a writing card's first character before a stroke, a hint, the outline or Animate; a tone card's first character before any answer; a meaning or pinyin card before its answer, `recall-back-button`) goes back to the card graded before it in this sitting of Review, and further back up to `LOOK_BACK_LIMIT` (10); **at the end of Review**, "‹ Back to the last card" (`look-back-button`). Not in a lesson, Learn or Free practice. Options `backToCard` of `wordFlow`, `toneFlow`, `recallCard`; `graded`, `lookBackAt` in `src/review.js`.
  - **What shows:** "Writing · 1 back" or "· 3 back" (short, or a sentence's label took two lines on a phone), and on the face (`showPastCard` in `src/pastCard.js`) a writing card's **raw input**, each character in a square (as many to a row as make a square of them) green or red with ✓ or ✕, a copy taken as it was finished (`quiz.drawing()`, `wordFlow`'s `drawings()`; kept in memory, never saved); skipped characters and writing without raw input show the character itself. A tone card shows its characters in such squares. Meaning and pinyin cards show the word, its reading and meaning. Under it, the grades as they were at the time (`gradeOptions` of the card before), the grade given pressed (`aria-pressed`).
  - **Correcting:** another grade, or on a writing card of several characters or any tone card a tap on a square (its verdict turns and the grade becomes the one then suggested, saved at once), **saves the review again under its own ID and time** (`correct`), with the card's schedule worked out from the card as it was before (`cardBefore`), as if that had been the answer. The one change ever made to a saved review (the owner: "this is a correction, not an addition"); combining backups is unaffected since no other device can have it yet. **Only while it is the card's latest review** (`canCorrect`): an Again card that came back and was graded again keeps it ("Graded again since, so this grade stays.", the grades disabled). `suggestedGrade`, `duration` and the streak stay as they were; a card suspended as a leech stays suspended.
  - **Going on:** a grade, ›, or a tap on the face goes to the card after; past the newest, Review shows the next card afresh (the card left was untouched, so nothing is lost).

## Learn and the lesson

- **A word is waiting or learned** (`isLearned` in `src/session.js`): learned once through Learn (`learnedAt`) or once it has any studied card. Adding a word only puts it in line.
- **Review only shows learned words.** `planSession` skips the rest and reports how many are waiting.
- **Learn is opened** from a deck (a tap on its row while words wait, `mainAction` in `src/decksPanel.js`; "Learn new words" on its page), from the menu while words are waiting (`learn-tab`, with `waitingToLearn`, on the deck chosen in Review; the owner, 2026-10-16), and from Review with nothing due (`learn-next-button`). With no words left to learn, Review and Learn offer "Add words" (`add-words-button`). **With no words at all** (someone who skipped the introduction; roadmap item 2, the owner, 2026-10-19: lead a clueless newcomer after the introduction, not inside it), "You have no words yet." comes with "Start with HSK 1 (3.0)" or "Start with TOCFL Novice 1" as the main button (`start-level-button`; the level from `startingLevel` in `src/decks.js`, the introduction's rule), "Add my own words" (the same button, plain) and the link "Choose another level" (Decks › Ready-made decks). The level is added with `addReadyLevels` ("Adding words…") and Learn opens on its first word (`offerAdding` in `src/review.js`, handlers in `src/main.js`; `tests/browser/startLevel.browser.js`). Someone who chose "None" in the introduction still ends in Decks. Tests open it with `openLearn`.
- **Learn** takes waiting words one at a time in `order` (`nextToLearn`) and teaches each with the lesson; finishing marks it learned and its cards come up in Review as new, a few minutes apart. Between two words: "大 is learned: its cards will come up in Review.", with "Learn the next word".
- **"New words per day" is a goal, not a limit** (the owner; counts words, not cards; default 5): "3 of 5 learned today", said once (2026-10-18). It still limits writing cards started again after a change of script.
- **The lesson** (`lessonSteps` in `src/session.js`): a look at the word; its tones, as practice; writing over the outline with each next stroke shown; over the bare outline; from memory. A word of several characters goes through each step whole. In the outline steps the boxes show the characters still to write, faintly; from memory they are "·". With writing off, or no stroke data, it ends after the tones.
- **Each next stroke is shown until written** (the owner didn't want the whole character animated first): `showDemo` in `src/quiz.js` draws it in blue over and over along its middle line (`.stroke-demo-line`), our own layer since Hanzi Writer can't animate during a quiz. `wordFlow` asks with `demoStrokes` and `showOutline`.
- **Nothing in Learn is graded** (the owner, 2026-10-16): the last step is practice, judged so that writing it well turns green, but nothing is saved; all cards start in Review. (Words learned before then keep the graded review of their last step.)
- **Starting again in a practice step wipes the slate** (`freshRestart` in `src/wordFlow.js`; the owner, 2026-10-16): every step of Learn and the tracing steps of a lesson in Review. The graded step in Review still counts every attempt. **A character right only after starting again is amber** (tester 2; the owner, 2026-10-18: "you got it right, but not on the first try"): starting again after a mistake or hint counts a try (`retriesPerChar`), and its box shows "✓2" in amber (`.slot.passed.retried`), "No mistakes · 2nd try", the printed character amber (`--reference-retried`). Still nothing saved.
- **"I know this word: skip its lesson"** (every step; the owner, 2026-10-13: "the app should trust me"): the word's new cards are answered Easy (`markedKnown`) and the next word starts at once. It counts in "learned today".
- **In Review**, a new writing card of a word not taught in Learn (`taughtInLearn`: older words, or one to write again in the other script) gets the lesson without the first look, its last step graded. The `lessons` setting ("In Review, teach before testing") switches that off. Known gap: a word learned in one script and switched before its writing card was reviewed gets no lesson in the new script.
- **Repeating a lesson** (tester 2's R6; the owner, 2026-10-18): "Repeat lesson" on a learned word's page opens the mode `'lesson'` (`repeatLesson` in `src/main.js`, `startRepeat` in `src/review.js`): Learn's lesson for that word alone, as practice. **Nothing is saved**, and no "I know this word" (`current.repeating`). "Repeat lesson · step 1 of 5", first look "Have another look."; no deck chooser or counts (`countsRow`). "‹ Back" returns to the word's page. At the end, "Lesson done." with Repeat lesson and Back to the word (`lesson-back-button`). `tests/browser/repeatLesson.browser.js`.
- **Review and Learn are one piece of code** (`createReview`; `activate('learn')`). `activate` waits for `review.load()`, so a card is never chosen from stale words. `tests/browser/lesson.browser.js` covers Learn and the lesson.

## How long studying takes

Built on 2026-10-19 (roadmap item 4); shown on the Progress tab (below).

- **Each review in Review saves `duration`**, in milliseconds: from the card coming up until a grade is tapped (the owner, 2026-10-19: as Anki does, so imported reviews, which carry Anki's own `duration`, add up with ours). A new writing card's lesson in Review counts toward its review.
- **Each word learned in Learn saves `learnDuration`** on the word (the owner, 2026-10-19: Learn's time counts too), from its first step until the lesson ends. Carried over by the word's panel; combining backups keeps it with the earliest `learnedAt` (`src/merge.js`).
- **A step counts at most a minute** (`MAX_STEP_MS` in `src/timing.js`, Anki's default "maximum answer seconds"; fixed, no setting). A lesson's steps are counted one by one (`lap`), each up to the limit, so a five-step lesson can count five minutes.
- **Time out of sight doesn't count:** the stopwatch pauses while the page is hidden (`visibilitychange`: another app or tab, the screen locked).
- **Not counted:** "I know this word" (also any lesson steps before it), Free practice, a repeated lesson, and a card left unanswered when another tab is opened. Reviews before 2026-10-19 just lack the field.
- `createStopwatch` in `src/timing.js` (tested in `tests/timing.test.js`), used in `src/review.js`.

## Writing cards per script

The owner, 2026-10-13: a learner switching scripts keeps their words, with the characters that differ studied as new.

- **Writing cards are kept per script; the others are not** (`PER_SCRIPT_TYPES`). A word written the same in both has one writing card.
- **Card IDs:** `cardIdFor(word, type, script)`: traditional is the plain `<wordId>#writing`, simplified `<wordId>#writing#simplified`; `allCardIds(word)` lists both.
- **After a switch**, a writing card known only in the other script is learned again (`toLearnAgain` in `planSession`): it counts toward new words, comes before words never seen, and starts from scratch. The other card stays, so switching back loses nothing. A word's own script ("Cards in") works the same (`scriptOf(word)`).
- **Old cards** under the plain ID are moved to the simplified ID if their latest review was simplified (`fileCardsByScript` in `src/scriptCards.js`; at start-up while `cardsFiledByScript` is missing, again after restoring an older backup, and when a word is saved in its panel). Reviews keep their `cardId`.
- **Correcting the traditional form or the pinyin** in the pop-up panel changes the word's ID: `renameWord` in `src/db.js` moves the word, cards and reviews in one transaction.
- **Not handled:** making the two forms the same again leaves the `#simplified` card unused; correcting traditional forms in bulk.

## Leeches and suspended cards

- **A leech** is a card graded Again at least `leechLapses` times after being learned (default 6, the owner's number; 2 to 30), counted from ts-fsrs's own `lapses`, so it survives rebuilding from reviews (`isLeech`, `lapsesOf` in `src/leeches.js`).
- **`leechAction`** (the owner, 2026-10-13): `flag` (default, only pointed out) or `suspend`. The owner wants as many scheduling options as Anki in the long run.
- **Pointed out** in Review by **"Leech" beside the kind of card, once the card is answered** (`leech-tag`, `showLeechTag` in `src/review.js`; the owner, 2026-10-26, from two drafts: the note and "Suspend this card" under the grades made the page scroll on the tablet). **Not before:** that a card keeps being forgotten is a hint in itself (the owner). A tap opens the word's page with this card's strip, which says "Leech: you have forgotten this card 14 times. Words, in the menu, lists your leeches." and has Suspend. In the top line's 36px (24px tall), the smallest size under 390px wide like the kind of card. Also in the "Show" list (`word-filter`: All words, Leeches, Words with a suspended card). A leech's row says what is forgotten: "writing · forgotten 7 times · 習 missed 5 of 9 times".
- **Two exports:** "Download list" (TSV) and "Copy as AI prompt" (`leechPrompt`, asking for component breakdowns and mnemonics; to a file where the clipboard isn't allowed). Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Suspended kinds are stored on the word** (`suspended`), not the card, since cards are rebuilt from reviews when combining. Writing cards in both scripts suspend together. `hasCardType` keeps them out of Review; history and schedule stay.
- **Suspend and Resume** on each row of the Cards fold-out on a word's page (`setSuspended` in `src/cardsPanel.js`; the owner, 2026-10-17): the row says "Suspended", the heading "Cards (1 suspended)". Buttons not colored (red and green mean missed and passed) and rows not faded (contrast).
- **With `suspend`, a card is suspended at the review that makes it a leech** (`becameLeech`), so a resumed one is suspended again the next time it is forgotten. A line above the next card says so (`review-notice`).

## Managing cards

- **The Cards fold-out on a word's page** opens with a line on what a card is (`page.cardsExplained`; tester 4 found "Cards" confusing, 2026-10-21), then lists its cards in the script it is studied in (and a writing card only in the other script): next due, reviews, times forgotten, suspended; "Start over" on each studied card, "Suspend"/"Resume" on each, and "I know this word" (`cardsOfWord` in `src/cardManager.js`, `showCardsOf` in `src/cardsPanel.js`). They act at once (`onWordChanged`).
- **From a card in Review** (the owner, 2026-10-17): "About this word" on an answered card opens the word's page with a strip for that card, "This card: Pinyin · forgotten 4 times", with Suspend and Start over (`showThisCard` in `src/wordPage.js`; `thisCard` in `openAbout`, `src/review.js`). Either takes the card out ungraded; the page closes, a line says what was done, the next card comes. Start over asks first. Not from Words, Free practice, or once graded. **The strip is behind About, not Edit** (the owner, 2026-10-18): Edit is a form waiting for Save, these act at once.
- **"About this word" is inside every answered card**, at its bottom left (2026-10-18; `showCardAbout`, `card-about-button`): over the writing area's bottom edge, inside the face of the other kinds, "About this sentence" on a sentence. A learning note brings its own link. Hidden while practicing again.
- **Start over** removes the card and notes the time in the word's `startedOverAt`, by kind (`withStartedOver`, `startCardsOver` in `src/db.js`). Reviews before it stop counting (`countedFrom` in `mergeCards`); combining keeps the later time. Both scripts' writing cards together.
- **"I know this word"** is an ordinary Easy review on each still-new card of the kinds on (`markedKnown`, `saveKnown`; reviews carry `markedKnown: true`). An Easy first answer comes back in about 8 days, so the claim is checked.
- **Setting a due date by hand was left out** (the owner, on Claude's advice): it fights the scheduler, needs its own mark to survive combining, and suspending covers "not now".
- **Order:** learning order, next due first, most forgotten first.
- **Select words** (in both lists, the Words tab's and a deck's page's): taps pick rows (a blue edge) and a bar offers Select all listed (all the list's words, not only the 200 shown), I know these words, Suspend, Resume, Start over, Remove (these two ask first), Add to a deck, and on a deck's list **"Move to this deck"** (tester 2's R4, 2026-10-18; `getListDeck`: the words leave that deck and every deck inside it). **Holding a row half a second starts selecting** (R3; `whenHeld` in `src/wordRows.js`, cancelled by moving 10px; the tap ending the hold is swallowed). **Selecting ends on leaving the list** (`stopSelecting`; the owner, 2026-10-18), and the bar's chooser is refilled after every change (`afterChange`). Groups inside ready-made levels are left out of it.
- **Not built:** a row per card; taking several words out of a deck at once; undo.

## Streaks

Built on 2026-10-19 (roadmap item 3; the owner chose each part that day).

- **A day counts once enough was studied:** by default 10 different cards reviewed or 2 words learned in Learn, whichever comes first (`streakCards`, `streakWords`; Settings › Daily study, "A day counts after … cards reviewed or … words learned"). A day is a study day, from 4:00 (`studyDayKey`).
- **A missed day breaks it; the best is kept** and shown. Before today counts, the streak up to yesterday still stands until 4:00.
- **It can be switched off** (`streaks`, on by default; the owner: "all nudges should be able to be turned off in the settings for people who don't want to be productively emotionally manipulated by their app"). Off, there is no streak screen and no line in Review; how much makes a day count is hidden too.
- **Nothing is stored:** the streak is worked out from the reviews' times and the words' `learnedAt` (`studyByDay`, `streakAt` in `src/streak.js`, tested), so combining backups and the Anki import carry it over, and Anki's history counts. Changing how much counts changes past days too. **Counted quickly after each grade** (the owner, 2026-10-24, with 186,000 reviews from Skritter: recounting them all after every grade kept the next card from responding): Review counts the days before today once a study day (`studyBefore` in `streakNow`, `src/review.js`; again when `load()` reads the reviews afresh) and only today's reviews and words each time; `streakAt` counts each run back once, from its last day (`nextDayKey`). **The reviews are read after the first card is up** (2026-10-26; `readReviews`, `reviewsRead` in `src/review.js`, a part at a time with `getAllInParts` in `src/db.js`): reading them first left the writing area empty for seconds on the owner's tablet. Until they are read, a grade's pill waits for them, the screen waiting from before shows once they are read unless the card was touched (else at the next pause), the end of Review waits for them, and "What are tones?" is not offered. Reviews saved meanwhile are added to those read (`noteReviews`).
- **Marking a word as known isn't studying:** reviews with `markedKnown` and the `learnedAt` set at the same moment count for nothing. Free practice and a repeated lesson save nothing, so they don't count either.
- **When it is said: not in the middle of studying** (the owner, 2026-10-19: the screen opening as soon as the day counted interrupted Review and Learn). After a grade or a word learned that makes the day count (`noteStreak` in `src/review.js`):
  - **the first streak screen ever on the device opens at once**, so a new learner meets streaks, and the switch to turn them off, when it first happens;
  - **after that, the streak pill** (`streak-pill`): "🌕🐇 12 days in a row ✨", small, floating over the top bar (nothing on the page moves), gone by itself after 4 seconds (longer while the mouse rests on it or it has the keyboard's focus), in a `role="status"` box so screen readers say it without moving there. **A tap on it opens the streak screen.** Once a study day, kept on the device (`streak-pill-shown` in `localStorage`, `streak-pill-shown-test` for the test copy; in memory only until 2026-10-24, when a reload brought it back the same day for the owner); never if the screen was already seen today;
  - **the screen then waits for a pause** (`showStreakIfWaiting`): the end of Review ("All done for now"), the end of today's words in Learn (the goal reached or nothing left; `atEnd`), **opening the menu** (the screen comes first, the menu once it is closed; the owner preferred this to switching tabs), or opening Review or Learn again the same study day (after the app was closed, say). If the day counts right at the end of Review or Learn, the screen opens at once, without the pill.
  - "Waiting" is only "today counts and `streak-shown` isn't today", so nothing more is stored.
- **The streak screen** (`streak-dialog`, `src/streakScreen.js`): a pop-up at one of the moments above. Once a day per device (`streak-shown` in `localStorage`, `streak-shown-test` for the test copy); not over another pop-up (it waits for the next pause). **Two kinds** (the owner, 2026-10-19, from pictures: the first draft, a number and "Today counts:", didn't say what it was to someone who didn't know the app had streaks):
  - **the first ever on the device** (nothing in `streak-shown` yet) explains: "NEW: STREAKS", the number, "days in a row!" ("day studied!" for 1), "Each day you review 10 cards or learn 2 words, your streak grows by one. Miss a day and it starts again." (the numbers from the settings), "Got it", and the quiet "Don't count streaks", which switches streaks off at once (`streak-off`);
  - **after that, short:** "STUDY STREAK", the number, "Your streak is safe for today. Keep going! ✨" (**not "Today is done"**: the owner, since the day's minimum is small and studying on is the point), and the best, quieter ("Your best: 20 days.", "Your best yet! ⭐", or "A new start. Your last streak was 5 days."), with Continue.
  - Celebratory (the owner): emoji, "!", **a pop as it opens** (`streak-pop`: the rabbit, then the number, grow past their size and settle), and **sparkles in the number's color** (`--accent`) rising from behind it (`throwSparkles`, `SPARKLES`, `streak-sparkle`): nine four-pointed stars in an even fan over the top of the number, near and far, big and small by turns, each twinkling, rising and fading in 0.6 seconds, removed once done (the owner, 2026-10-19, after confetti scattered all around: "more like sparkles … upwards and outwards … faster"). Both left out where the device asks for less motion. Emoji are marked `aria-hidden`.
- **The mascot's place** (the rabbit to be commissioned, `docs/history.md`): `streak-mascot` above the kicker, for now 🌕🐇 (the Jade Rabbit of the name; the owner chose it over 🔥 and 🎉 as the nicest to replace with the drawing), with `data-mood` (`happy`, or `fresh` for a new start after a broken streak) saying which picture to show once there is one. - **The end of Review says it** (`streakLine`): "Streak: 3 days in a row. Your best: 12 days." once today counts; before, "To keep your 3-day streak, review 7 more cards or learn 2 more words today." Nothing while there is no streak.
- **The browser tests start with streaks off** (the harness, as with tips); `tests/browser/streak.browser.js` turns them on.
- **Not built:** reminders while the app is closed (the owner, 2026-10-19: left out; without a server a browser can't reliably remind, and Chrome's periodic background sync is for an installed app on Android only, on Chrome's timing); a streak in the top bar. The calendar of days studied is on the Progress tab (below).

## The Progress tab

Built on 2026-10-19 (the owner chose each part that day, layout A of two drawn side by side, "boxes"). "Progress" in the menu, after Words (`progress-tab`, `progress-panel`; `src/progressPanel.js` draws, `src/progress.js` counts, tested in `tests/progress.test.js` and `tests/browser/progress.browser.js`). **Nothing is stored for it**: everything is worked out from the reviews, the cards and the words when the tab is opened, as the streak is, so backups and the Anki import carry it over.

- **In order:** Today; Your cards; the range; Streak (or "Days studied"); Time studied; Words and characters.
- **Today:** four numbers in boxes: cards reviewed and words learned (counted as the streak counts them, `studyByDay`), time studied, and the share of today's answers that weren't "Again".
- **Your cards:** a row a kind of card switched on, and "All cards", each a bar from new to mature (`cardStages`): **new** (not studied yet, if the word can have it: `hasCardType` from `src/review.js`, which leaves out suspended cards), **learning** (FSRS learning or relearning), **young** (next due under 21 days after its last review, `MATURE_DAYS`, as in Anki) and **mature**. The writing card counted is the one in the word's script.
- **The range** (`RANGES`): Month (the last 30 days), 3 months, Year, All (from the first day studied), as in Anki (the owner). It sits above the three parts it changes; Today and Your cards always show now.
- **Streak:** the streak and the best (`streakAt`), then the days studied, darker for more cards and words learned (four shades relative to the busiest day in the range), a day not studied an empty square, today edged. A month is a calendar with dates, a row a week from Monday; longer ranges a strip of small squares, a column a week, as in Anki (the owner: the small squares are fine). **With streaks off, the heading is "Days studied" and the number is left out** (nudges can be switched off).
- **Time studied:** bars of a day (Month), a week counted back from today (3 months), or a calendar month (Year, All) (`barSize`, `groupDays`), from the reviews' `duration` and the words' `learnDuration` (`timeByDay`); minutes, or hours once a bar holds two. Under it, the total in the range, "per day studied" (an average over the days with time recorded, not every day of the range), and all time. Above each chart, "Tap a bar for its time.", "Tap a day for its numbers.", "Tap the chart for a day's numbers." (the owner, 2026-10-19: not "to see it"; the bar is already seen). No note that times were recorded only from 2026-10-19 (the owner, 2026-10-19: there were no real users before).
- **Words and characters** (the owner's addition, 2026-10-19): two lines of running totals (`growthByDay`, `growthOver`), words learned in jade and characters known in violet (`--chart-second`; amber and blue were too close to the jade for color-blind eyes, checked with a color-vision simulation), each total named at its line's end, with a key and "+19 words and +31 characters in the last 30 days." The scale starts from zero for a year or all, near the lowest value for a month or 3 months, so a month's growth shows. The key says 詞 or 词 by the learner's script.
  - **A word** is a learned word (`learnedAt`; a word from before the Learn tab or from Anki: its first card's first review, `learnedAtOf`), "I know this word" included, **counted by its characters**: 行 xíng and 行 háng are one (the owner). Sentences and radicals are left out.
  - **A character** is any Chinese character in a learned word (not the OK of 卡拉OK), counted once, from the day the first word with it was learned, **in the learner's script** (`settings.script`): someone learning both counts 們 and 们 once.
  - **The switch "Split 简体字/繁體字"** beside the heading (`progress-apart`, saved as `charactersApart`, off at first; the owner, 2026-10-19), **shown only with a "Both" script setting**: on, a word's characters count in the script its cards are in, and in the other script from the day its writing card there was first reviewed, so someone who has written 們 and 们 knows two. Its label is "Split 简/繁" where the full one would drop below the heading (`fitApartLabel`: a small phone, large text); "简体字/繁體字" alone read as choosing which script's numbers to see, and "Separate simp./trad." didn't fit (the owner, 2026-10-19). Screen readers hear "Count simplified and traditional forms as different characters".
- **Tapping a day or a bar shows its numbers** on a line above the chart (the owner: yes), not in a bubble a finger would cover: "Sun, Oct 19: 42 cards, 3 words learned, 14 min." A mouse resting on it does too. Each chart takes the keyboard's focus and the arrow keys move along it (Home, End, Page Up and Down too). **To a screen reader each chart is a slider** (`role="slider"` in `makeExplorable`, `src/progressPanel.js`; tester 4, 2026-10-21, found the tab hard with one): its name, and the day or bar it is on as the same sentence (`aria-valuetext`), today's to begin with; the arrow keys or a swipe up or down move it. The squares, bars and lines are hidden from it, and so is the line above (it would be said twice). The bars of Your cards say their numbers (`role="img"`). Not tried by hand with VoiceOver or TalkBack.
- **Drawn without a library:** the calendar and bars are plain elements, the lines SVG, all in the app's colors. The line chart is drawn as wide as its box, again when the window changes size.
- **Not built:** choosing one deck; Anki's other charts (intervals, hours of the day, answer buttons, cards due ahead); target retention (a setting of its own on the roadmap).
