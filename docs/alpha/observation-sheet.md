## While they work, note

- **Hesitations:** where they stopped and looked around, and for how long.
- **Wrong turns:** where they tapped something expecting something else. What did they expect?
- **Words they used** for things ("the list", "my cards", "the menu"): these show what the app's own labels should say.
- **Things they did not notice** that were on screen.
- **Anything they said aloud** that was a judgment: "oh nice", "what?", "where is...".
- **Writing:** strokes marked wrong that looked right to you, and how they reacted.

## At the end, ask

1. What was the most confusing moment?
2. What did you expect to find that was not there?
3. Would you open it again tomorrow? Why, or why not?
