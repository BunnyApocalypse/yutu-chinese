# Things to try

These are goals, not instructions. How to do each one is for you to find, and where you cannot find it, please tell me.

## First day

1. Open the app and get to the point where you could start studying.
2. If you are new to Chinese: find out what pinyin and tones are before your first lesson.
3. Get some words into the app that suit your level. (There are ready-made lists, and you can add your own.)
4. Learn your first three new words.

## First week

5. Study every day for a few minutes: whatever the app says is due, and a few new words.
6. Add one word that is not in any list: something from your own textbook or life.
7. Find a word you learned earlier and look at what the app knows about it.
8. Change something about how the app behaves to suit you (anything: sound, which kinds of cards you get, how many new words a day).
9. Hear how a word is pronounced.
10. Change your mind about a card you just answered: give it a different grade, or tell the app it judged a character wrongly.
11. Find out how much you have studied this week.

## Second week

12. Notice a word you keep getting wrong. See whether the app helps you with it.
13. Make sure your words are kept safe on this device, and make a backup of your progress.
14. If you have a second device: get your progress onto it.
15. Add a sentence to study, if that appeals to you.
16. If you use Anki: bring one of your decks over, your own or a shared one.

If you only do 1 to 5, that is already useful. Tell me which ones you skipped, and why: "I did not want to" is a fine answer and worth knowing.
