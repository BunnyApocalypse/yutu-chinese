# Things to try

These are goals, not instructions. How to do each one is for you to find, and where you cannot find it, please tell me.

## First day

1. Open the app and get to the point where you could start studying.
2. Get some words into the app that suit your level. (There are ready-made lists, and you can add your own.)
3. Learn your first three new words.

## First week

4. Study every day for a few minutes: whatever the app says is due, and a few new words.
5. Add one word that is not in any list: something from your own textbook or life.
6. Find a word you learned earlier and look at what the app knows about it.
7. Change something about how the app behaves to suit you (anything: sound, which kinds of cards you get, how many new words a day).
8. Hear how a word is pronounced.

## Second week

9. Notice a word you keep getting wrong. See whether the app helps you with it.
10. Make a backup of your progress.
11. If you have a second device: get your progress onto it.
12. Add a sentence to study, if that appeals to you.

If you only do 1 to 4, that is already useful. Tell me which ones you skipped, and why: "I did not want to" is a fine answer and worth knowing.
