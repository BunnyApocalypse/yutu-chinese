# Thanks for trying 玉兔中文

玉兔中文 (Yùtù Zhōngwén, "Jade Rabbit Chinese") is an app for learning to read and write Chinese characters: it shows you a word's meaning and you write the word, stroke by stroke, with your finger or a stylus, and it brings each word back just before you would forget it. It also has cards for meanings, pinyin and tones. You don't need to know any Chinese to start.

This is an early test version made by one person. I am asking you to use it for about two weeks, ten minutes a day, and tell me where it got in your way.

## Getting it

Open this address in your phone's or tablet's own browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox and so on): **https://bunnyapocalypse.github.io/yutu-chinese/**

If you tap the link inside another app (Discord, WhatsApp, an email app), it may open in that app's own little browser, which keeps the app's data apart and may lose it. Please copy the address into your real browser instead.

It then works without a connection. You can install it from the browser's menu ("Install app" or "Add to Home screen"), and it will behave like any other app.

**On an iPhone, iPad or Mac, please add it before you start studying:** on an iPhone or iPad with "Add to Home Screen" in Safari's Share menu, on a Mac with "Add to Dock" in Safari's File menu. Safari otherwise deletes the app's data after a week without use. The added app starts empty, so do this first.

## Three things to know

- **Your data stays on your device.** Nothing you enter is sent anywhere. That also means I cannot see it, and cannot get it back if it is lost.
- **Make a backup now and then.** In the app: the menu button (☰ at the top left), then Settings at the bottom, then "Your data and backups", then "Export backup". It saves one file. Keep it somewhere safe.
- **It changes while you use it.** After an update, a short "What's new" tells you what changed. I try hard not to lose anyone's progress, but this is a test version, and it could happen that an update asks you to start again.

## What I would like you to do

Just use it to study, as you would any such app. If you want somewhere to start, the list "Things to try" has a few ideas for the first days.

I am deliberately not explaining how the app works. If you cannot work something out, that is the app's fault, not yours, and it is exactly what I need to hear about.

## Telling me what you find

In the app, the menu (☰) has "Send feedback": it writes an email to **yutufeedback.delicious577@passmail.com** with your device and the app's version already filled in. If you have a GitHub account, you can instead open an issue at https://github.com/BunnyApocalypse/yutu-chinese/issues.

The most useful reports say three things: what you were trying to do, what you did, and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot. If the app ever shows a red line with "Details", please copy the details into your report.

Things I especially want to hear:

- a moment you did not know what to do next;
- something you looked for and did not find;
- writing that was marked wrong when you thought it was right, or right when it was wrong;
- anything that made you want to stop for the day;
- anything that looked broken;
- the app telling you your words might be lost, and whether it was clear what to do.

After two weeks I will send a short list of questions. Thank you: this is a real help.
