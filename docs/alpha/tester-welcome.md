# Thanks for trying 玉兔中文

玉兔中文 (Yùtù Zhōngwén, "Jade Rabbit Chinese") is an app for learning to read and write Chinese characters: it shows you a word's meaning and you write the word, stroke by stroke, with your finger or a stylus, and it brings each word back just before you would forget it. It also has cards for meanings, pinyin and tones.

This is an early test version made by one person. I am asking you to use it for about two weeks, ten minutes a day, and tell me where it got in your way.

## Getting it

Open this address in the browser on your phone or tablet: **https://bunnyapocalypse.github.io/hanzi-trainer/**

It then works without a connection. You can add it to your home screen from the browser's menu, and it will behave like any other app. **On an iPhone or iPad, please do add it to the Home Screen:** otherwise the browser may delete the app's data after a week without use.

## Three things to know

- **Your data stays on your device.** Nothing you enter is sent anywhere. That also means I cannot see it, and cannot get it back if it is lost.
- **Make a backup now and then.** In the app: the menu button (☰ at the top left), then Settings, then "Your data and backups", then "Export backup". It saves one file. Keep it somewhere safe.
- **It may change while you use it.** I will try hard not to lose anyone's progress, but this is a test version, and it could happen that an update asks you to start again.

## What I would like you to do

Just use it to study, as you would any such app. If you want somewhere to start, `tasks.md` lists a few things to try in the first days.

I am deliberately not explaining how the app works. If you cannot work something out, that is the app's fault, not yours, and it is exactly what I need to hear about.

## Telling me what you find

Email **hanzifeedback.veggie892@passmail.com**, or, if you have a GitHub account, open an issue at https://github.com/BunnyApocalypse/hanzi-trainer/issues. The same address is in the app: the cog wheel, then "Help".

The most useful reports say three things: what you were trying to do, what you did, and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot. Please say which device and browser you use.

Things I especially want to hear:

- a moment you did not know what to do next;
- something you looked for and did not find;
- writing that was marked wrong when you thought it was right, or right when it was wrong;
- anything that made you want to stop for the day;
- anything that looked broken.

After two weeks I will send a short list of questions. Thank you: this is a real help.
