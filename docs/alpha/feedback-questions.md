# After two weeks: a few questions

Short answers are fine. Skip any you have nothing to say about.

## About you

1. How long have you been learning Chinese, and which characters (simplified, traditional, both)?
2. Which device and browser did you use? Finger or stylus?
3. What have you used before to learn vocabulary or characters?

## How it went

4. On how many of the fourteen days did you open it? What made you skip the days you skipped?
5. What was the most confusing moment? What were you trying to do?
6. Was there something you looked for and never found?
7. Did the app ever mark your writing wrong when you thought it was right, or the other way round? Which characters, if you remember?
8. How did the amount of work each day feel: too little, about right, too much?
9. When you learned a new word, did the lesson (the steps before the first real test) feel too long, too short, or about right?
10. Did you use the sound? Was it good enough?
11. Did you open a word's page (its notes, example sentence, the parts of its characters)? Was anything on it useful, wrong or odd?
12. Did anything look broken, or behave differently from one day to the next?

## Overall

13. If this were finished, would you use it instead of what you use now? What would have to be true for that?
14. What is the one thing you would change first?
15. Is there anything you liked that I should be careful not to break?

## Optional

16. If you made a backup or moved your progress to another device: how did that go?
17. Anything else.
