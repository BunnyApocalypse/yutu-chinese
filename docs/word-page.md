# The word page, sentences and sound

## The word page

- **A read-only `<dialog>`** (`word-page`, `src/wordPage.js`): the word in the script(s) learned, its reading with a loudspeaker, its meaning, its decks (below), learning note and example sentence, and each character with its parts. Its buttons: Practice, Repeat lesson (or Learn it now), Edit, Remove.
- **The top is the characters, the pinyin and the meaning, bigger than the rest** (tester 4, 2026-10-21; pictures `local/tester4/word-page-before.png` and `-after.png`): the reading at `--size-big`, the meaning of one of your words centered at `--size-title` (`.page-meaning.own`), the deck boxes after it. A dictionary word's page lists each reading with its meanings as before.
- **Where it opens:** a tap on a word in a list (`showWord` in `src/wordsPanel.js`; the pop-up panel for changing the word is behind Edit), and "About this word" inside an answered card (`docs/cards.md`).
- **Remove** (`word-page-remove`; tester 2; the owner, 2026-10-18): the same question as in the word's panel (`removeAsked` in `src/wordDialog.js`); the page closes once the word is gone.
- **"Repeat lesson"** (`word-page-lesson`; 2026-10-18): a learned word's lesson again, as practice (`docs/cards.md`). For a word still waiting it reads **"Learn it now"** and opens its real lesson in Learn, first in line, on the listed deck if the word is in it, else its first deck (`learnWordNow` in `src/wordsPanel.js`); not when "Learn it now" is already at the top (a word just added). Not on a sentence's page.
- **Practice, Remove and Repeat lesson are not on a page opened in Review or Learn** (`canPractice`): leaving would lose the card or lesson.
- **Everything tappable leads on:** a word of the example sentence, a character, a part. Each opens its own page, with Back to return (`trail`). The parts open at once (big buttons).
- **A word of a sentence or note shows a small popup first** (`word-peek`, `peek`; 2026-10-15, after a tester tapped one by accident): the word, its reading in that sentence, its meaning (the learner's own, else the dictionary's for that reading) on one line, a loudspeaker, and "About this word". A `popover="auto"`, so a stray tap costs nothing; **the tap that closes it does nothing else** (a capture-phase listener): it doesn't reveal an answer or open what it landed on.
- **The words of a sentence at the top of its own page can be tapped too** (the owner, 2026-10-18; `showHeading` with `fillSentence`, `sentenceWords`). One typed without pinyin is split into single characters with their usual readings.
- **The words of a learning note can be tapped** (the owner, 2026-10-15, in place of pinyin in beginners' notes), on the page and in a card's tip: `tappableText` makes the Chinese into buttons (`note-word`) once the dictionary is there; `splitIntoWords` in `src/dictionary.js` takes the longest known word at each point (我们 / 的 / 老师). With no sentence to give the reading, the popup shows the learner's own word, else the usual reading (`likeliestEntry`): 的 is "de". A word and the punctuation after it stay on one line; the words are as tall as the text, not 48px.
- **A page for something not yet yours** shows the dictionary's entries and, **in the bar at the pop-up's bottom edge, always in view, "Add to my words" with the deck beside it** (`word-page-add`, `showAdding`; tester 4, 2026-10-21, added the example sentence, whose Add was the one in view, and took it for the word; the owner chose this, B of two, and the label without the word's characters so it fits a phone's width, 2026-10-22; pictures `local/tester4/add-*.png`). The deck box narrows before the line wraps. With a choice of reading where it has several (the one tapped first, `target.entry`, else the likeliest); `addEntry` gives it the dictionary's meaning; the decks offered are the learner's own (`deckChooser`). Once added, it becomes the word's own page. It is the learner's word only if the reading matches (行 xíng is not 行 háng).
- **The deck boxes** under a word's heading and under a studied example sentence (`deckChips`; the owner, 2026-10-18), after "Deck:" or "Decks:". **A tap opens that deck's page** (`chip-button`, `--tap-least`), and "‹ Back" then returns to the word's page where it was opened from (`cameFromWord`, `goBackToWord`). Plain boxes in Review or Learn.
- **A card's tip** (`src/cardTip.js`; the owner, 2026-10-14, so learners find the page): at a new word's first look and under the answer of a meaning or pinyin card, the start of the note (three lines), the example sentence with its English, and "About this word". The face then scrolls from the top (`.card-face.with-tip`). **The word, its pinyin and its meaning stay bigger than the tip** (tester 4, 2026-10-21; the owner chose B of two, 2026-10-22; pictures `local/tester4/face-*.png`): the word at its full size, the meaning bold at `--size-title` (`.face-meaning`, on every card's answer), the tip's sentence at `--size-body`; and **always in that order**, word, pinyin, meaning (`showAnswer` in `src/recallCard.js`; until then a meaning card put its meaning first). On a small phone (375 by 667) the example sentence is below the fold and scrolls into view. `addTip` in `src/review.js` adds it once the notes are there, if the same card is still up (`noteOf`). Not on writing or tone cards.
- **The example sentence is split by the spaces in its pinyin** (`sentenceWords` in `src/notes.js`), so the notes' pinyin needs one space between words and none inside them. It shows in both scripts only when both are learned and differ, each labeled "trad." or "simp." at equal width so the lines align (`.sentence-script`; the owner, 2026-10-15).
- **Parts of characters come from data, not the notes** (`structureOf` in `src/characters.js`, Make Me a Hanzi): each part a button, labeled "meaning" or "sound" in a sound-meaning compound. Glosses for other kinds are often mnemonics, so the page words them "One way to see it" and "Seen as a picture", never as history.
- **"Usage checked by a native speaker"** (`page.checkedByNative`, `.note-check`; planned with the owner 2026-10-21, built 2026-10-24): a small tag under a learning note on the word page when its line in `data/notes/` has `native` in the eighth column (`checks` in `parseNotes`); a note without it shows nothing. The narrow wording is the owner's choice. Settings › Help has one line saying what the tag means (`settings.notesChecked`), and the credits say so too. Not on a card's tip (no room around the card's face). - **"Character facts from the sources"** (`page.checkedSources`; the wording the owner's, 2026-10-25): a second tag beside it when the line has `sources` (`data/notes/README.md`, "The source check"); both sit in one row, `.note-checks`. The same line in Settings › Help and the credits says what it means and that usage was not checked. Tags are added chunk by chunk as notes pass (`docs/history.md`, roadmap, "How sure a note is").
- **Notes are found by simplified characters and pinyin** (`noteFor`), whatever list the word is in; every file in `data/notes/` is read (`import.meta.glob`).
- **The two big files** (the notes and `data/makemeahanzi/dictionary.txt`, 2.5 MB) are fetched when a page is first opened and saved offline with the dictionary (`scripts/build-offline.js` saves `.tsv` and `.txt` assets).
- **The buttons work before the contents are there** (`start`), which the browser tests rely on; `data-shown` says what the filled page is about.
- **Not built:** statistics on the page; a character's stroke order; words that contain a character.

## Sentences

The owner agreed the outline on 2026-10-13 (a sentence stored as a word record; all three kinds of card possible) and hasn't tried it yet.

- **A sentence is a word record with `kind: 'sentence'`** (`src/sentences.js`), so decks, scheduling, leeches, suspending and combining need nothing new. ID `<traditional>|sentence`. Its `pinyin` is for reading only (tone marks, spaces, punctuation, or empty): `splitSyllables` finds no syllables in it, which keeps tone cards and recordings away; `formatPinyin` leaves it as it is.
- **Two cards, three things asked** (`sentenceCardTypes`): the `meaning` card is "reading" (`sentenceReading`, on by default); the `writing` card asks for the `cloze` word in a gap under the meaning ("我們是＿＿。") or the whole sentence (`sentenceWriting`: `word` default, `sentence`, `off`; no cloze word means whole). Review labels them "Sentence · reading" and "Sentence · writing". These settings are apart from "Card types", so `planSession` gets every kind and `hasCardType` decides.
- **A sentence is not taught**: it has `learnedAt` from the start, never waits in Learn, gets no lesson, doesn't count in "learned today".
- **Where they come from:** "Make cards from this sentence in [deck] Add sentence" under a word page's example sentence (the button named so since 2026-10-22, tester 4) (the page's word becomes the cloze word; both scripts stored), "Add a sentence" in "+ Add to deck", and an Anki import (`docs/anki.md`, "Sentences": a writing card for the whole sentence or none, suspended). Adding one already there puts it in the deck too. **Once the example sentence is yours, its deck boxes replace the chooser and Add** (`sentenceStudiedLine`; tester 2; the owner chose this on 2026-10-18 over hiding or greying).
- **Writing a sentence skips anything without stroke data**, punctuation included.
- **Its pop-up panel** has characters, pinyin (as typed), meaning, word to fill in (`edit-cloze`), decks and cards; not the Taiwan reading, tone readings or "Cards in". Changing the characters moves the cards and reviews (`renameWord`, `formerIds`).
- **Its sound is the device's own voice**; a device without a Chinese voice has none.
- **Not built:** sentences in a pasted list; ready-made sentences; choosing the cloze word by tapping; more than one cloze word; listening cards.

## Hearing a word

The owner chose the sources on 2026-10-13; the owner has listened and it works (2026-10-20).

- **Three ways, tried in order** (`planSpeech` in `src/speechPlan.js`):
  1. A recording of the word: about 8,500 words and characters (`data/audio-cmn/words`), named by characters, so used only when the reading asked for is the word's single listed `pinyin`.
  2. Its syllables back to back (the owner's idea; 1,679 toned syllables, `data/audio-cmn/syllables`): exactly the reading asked for, so a Taiwan reading is what is heard. `spokenSyllables` applies the tone changes dictionaries don't write (third before third, 不 before fourth, 一 except as a number). Silence at the ends is cut (`soundSpan` in `src/speech.js`).
  3. The device's own Chinese voice (`speechSynthesis`), if it works offline; it reads characters and can't be told the reading. Left for words with a neutral syllable and no recording (only 19 neutral syllables are recorded).
- **Coverage:** 80 to 95% of each level up to HSK 3.0 level 5, HSK 2.0 level 6 and TOCFL level 4 has its own recording; nearly all the rest can be put together.
- **The recordings are CC BY-SA** (`hugolpz/audio-cmn`, the 64k version, about 125 MB; the owner found the smaller two audibly compressed).
- **The loudspeaker** (`listen-button`) is beside the kind of card in Review and Learn (the owner: no "Listen" button, nothing that comes and goes in a lesson). `offerListen` shows it whenever hearing gives nothing away: all through a lesson; from the start on meaning and writing cards; on pinyin and tone cards once answered. `sound` setting: `auto` (default: spoken when an answer shows and at a new word's first look), `tap`, `off`. `data-spoken` (`word`, `syllables`, `voice`, `null`) tells the browser tests how it was spoken.
- **At a new word's first look nothing is spoken until something in Review or Learn was tapped** (`tappedSinceArriving`; the owner: not on switching to Learn). With `auto`, the word is also spoken after each lesson step (`speakAfterStep`). A browser keeps sound asleep until the page is tapped; `playRecordings` waits 300 ms and goes on.
- **Volume** (the owner, for reviewing with music on): a slider, `volume` 0 to 1, a device setting; letting go speaks 中文. Recordings go through a gain node with the value squared (half-way sounds half as loud).
- **Offline, recordings are the exception** (agreed with the owner): fetched when first played and kept in their own store (`offline-audio`); "Save all recordings for offline use" fetches all, in about 26 packs (`audioPacks`, `saveAudio` in `src/offline.js`), and `audioOffline` (a device setting) fetches newer versions at each start. **Never put them in `offline-data`**: with ten thousand files, its clean-up no longer finished within a minute. The service worker's app files leave them out (`scripts/build-offline.js`), or installing would fetch 125 MB.
- **Not built:** a speed setting; a choice of voice.
