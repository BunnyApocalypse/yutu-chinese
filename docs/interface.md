# The introduction, tips, menu, tabs and settings

## The introduction (first use)

- **Four short screens in one `<dialog>`** (`welcome` in `index.html`, `src/welcome.js`; the owner, 2026-10-16, after a tester found it far too wordy): **Welcome** (one sentence and the look), **Which characters are you learning?** ("What's the difference?" and "Pronunciation: …" are folds, `welcome-more`), **Select your starting level**, **Keep your words safe**. One or two lines each. All four kinds of card are on; Settings has them.
- **The pronunciation follows the script** until chosen by hand (`PRONUNCIATION_FOR_SCRIPT`: simplified → mainland, traditional → Taiwan, "both" → Accept both; the owner's choices).
- **The starting level** (`welcome-start`: HSK 3.0 level 1, TOCFL Novice 1, a deck from Anki, or nothing) is suggested from the script and pronunciation (traditional or Taiwan suggests TOCFL; a device with words gets nothing) until chosen by hand. Finishing adds it through the Decks tab's own code (`addReadyLevels`), "Adding words…" meanwhile, and ends in Learn; if the list can't be fetched, in Decks. A deck from Anki (the owner, 2026-10-19) ends in Decks with the import open (`docs/anki.md`); the last button then says "Import my Anki deck" unless it has storage to ask for.
- **The last screen has at most three buttons** (the tester's rule): Back, one main button that does what this browser needs, and "Not now", which saves the choices without asking. `welcomeStorage` in `src/storageAdvice.js` (tested) picks the main button and its line: "Install the app and start" where a Chromium browser offers its install question; **a Chromium browser without it** (`action: 'later'`; Chrome deciding by itself, the app already installed but opened in a tab, an offer turned down lately, Samsung Internet and others) **gets the steps as the screen, as Safari does** (the owner, 2026-10-19: no "Keep my words safe" where it can't do that, and not in small print): "Install 玉兔中文 as an app", why in one line, three numbered steps from the browser's menu (phone: ⋮, "Install app" or "Add to Home screen"; computer: ⋮ or ⋯, "Install", in Chrome under "Cast, save, and share"), and a plain "Start in the browser anyway", which quietly notes that the words are to be kept (`keepDataWanted`); no "Not now" (`welcome-keep-install-phone`, `welcome-keep-install-computer`; `addTo` `installPhone` or `installComputer`). Unlike Safari, the installed app shares the browser's data, so the words come along; elsewhere "Keep my words safe and start"; Firefox is told to choose "Allow" (at the top of the page on a computer, the bottom on a phone, the owner, 2026-10-16, and that Install in its menu gives an icon); **Safari** (not yet on the Home Screen or in the Dock) **gets the steps as the screen itself** (the owner, 2026-10-19: the most important text was quiet small print): "Add 玉兔中文 to your Home Screen" (iPhone, iPad) or "…to your Dock" (Mac), why in one line, three numbered steps in full-size text (iOS 26: Share may be under ⋯; "Open as Web App" left on; then open it from there and start there, where the app starts empty with the introduction again), and a plain, not main, "Start in Safari anyway" (`welcome-keep-home-screen`, `welcome-keep-dock`, `.welcome-steps`; `welcomeStorage` gives `addTo`). No button does it for them: only the person can, and "Keep my words safe" would seem to have done it. With storage already allowed or no way to ask, "Start learning" alone. The screen's text: "Your words are stored only in this browser, on this device.", with the line for this browser under it ("Allow it to keep them, so they are never deleted." dropped on 2026-10-19: in most browsers there is nothing to allow). Pictures of every browser: `local/data-safety-pictures/intro/` (`node local/shoot-welcome-storage.js`). Neither question is waited for.
- **Back and Next stay at the panel's bottom** while a screen scrolls (`.welcome-bar`; the owner, 2026-10-17), with a line above them only while something is hidden under them (`covering`, `showCovering`). "Skip the introduction" scrolls with the screen. Checked in `tests/browser/welcome.browser.js`.
- **It opens once per device** while `welcomeDone` is missing, starting from the settings as they are. Choices are saved only when the last screen is finished; "Skip the introduction" and Esc change nothing. "Show the introduction again" in Settings reopens it. Someone who skips starts with no words, and Review offers the suggested level to start with (`docs/cards.md`, "Learn and the lesson"; the owner, 2026-10-19). **The introduction itself is left alone** (the owner, 2026-10-19, after a draft with "Try writing a character" on its first screen: "it just interrupts the intro flow"; anything for newcomers comes after it).
- **It opens before the first tab is shown** (`startUp` in `src/main.js`); the browser tests rely on this: `startBrowsers` skips it, since a `<dialog>` would catch the strokes. `{ keepWelcome: true }` leaves it.
- **The scripts and pronunciations are explained where they are chosen** (the owner, 2026-10-13): where each script is used, a character that differs, links to Wikipedia's "Simplified" and "Traditional" (the owner preferred these to the article on the debate) and "Taiwanese Mandarin". The same text is behind a "?" beside Script and Pronunciation in Settings (any `.help-button` shows the element in its `aria-controls`, `src/settings.js`).

## Keeping the data: long-term storage

- **Asked for only after it is explained**: the introduction's last screen, and Settings under "Keeping your words" (the data group, `keep-data-status`; moved there from "Offline use" on 2026-10-19) while the answer is no, call `keepData.ask()` (`navigator.storage.persist()`, `src/offline.js`).
- **What the app says depends on the browser** (the owner, 2026-10-13): `browserKind` and `storageAdvice` in `src/storageAdvice.js` (tested). Firefox asks the person: expect the question, and after a refusal how to undo it. Chromium never asks and says yes for an installed app: install first. Safari decides by itself and deletes a site's data after about a week without a visit, **on a Mac too** (since Safari 13.1), unless the app is on the Home Screen or, on a Mac, in the Dock (File › Add to Dock, macOS 14 and later); the Mac's lines name the Dock (roadmap item 7, 2026-10-19). **An app put there starts with storage of its own** (only cookies come along), so the full advice says to bring words over with Export backup and Import backup (`BRING_OVER`). A phone or tablet is told from a computer by `isMobile` (`keepData.mobile`): an iPad's Safari names itself as a Mac, so a "Mac" with a touch screen is an iPad. Every refusal ends with "use Export backup".
- **"Install the app":** `src/offline.js` keeps Chromium's `beforeinstallprompt` event and shows the button (in Settings; the introduction's main button). The browser refuses unless something was just tapped. In Settings it also asks for long-term storage, so the installed app asks again by itself. **"Allow long-term storage" in Settings is shown only where it can help** (the owner, 2026-10-19: fewer buttons): not in Chrome before the app is installed, nor in Safari outside the Home Screen or the Dock.
- **Said again and again while the words may be lost** (`src/storageMessage.js`; the owner, 2026-10-19: "I would like to be annoying if the alternative is having users accidentally lose their data", in any browser; until then it was once per device): at the end of Review as it comes up (`reviewDone`, with words studied) and on the screen between two words in Learn (`learned`), every time, while `wordsAtRisk` (`src/storageAdvice.js`, tested) says so: Safari outside the Home Screen or the Dock (whatever it has promised); any other browser that hasn't promised to keep the data; one that can't be asked, until a backup, then a week after each (`RECENT_BACKUP_MS`). "Not now" puts it away until the app is next opened. **Off switch** (every nudge has one): "Stop the reminder in Review and Learn" under Settings › Keeping your words (named apart from the backup reminder's "Stop backup reminders", the owner, 2026-10-19) (`keep-reminder-button`, `storageReminderOff`, a device setting), after a warning, offered while the words are at risk. One line and two buttons (2026-10-18, the owner's wording chosen from four): "**Keep your words safe.** Install 玉兔中文 as an app so your browser doesn't delete them." with "Install the app" and "Not now"; Firefox's line to match (`storageMessageLine`, tested). **In a Chromium browser without its install question, "Keep my words safe" opens Settings** (`action: 'settings'`; the owner, 2026-10-19: before, it did nothing visible there; the owner's "Save my data" was dropped the same day, to keep the reminder's own words): "Install 玉兔中文 as an app or save a backup, so your browser doesn't delete them."; the button opens Settings › Your data and backups, which says how to install from the browser's menu and has Export backup, and notes `keepDataWanted`. If the install question comes later, the button becomes "Install the app" by itself. **In Safari and where nothing can be asked, the button is Export backup** (`action: 'export'`): Safari's line says it deletes after a week, how to add the app (iPhone: Share, or ⋯ then Share), and to bring the words over with a backup; after Export the line says where the file went and, in Safari, what to do next (`backup-made`). "Make sure" left out on purpose: installing makes keeping likely, not certain. After asking, the line becomes the full advice. Not a dot: Chrome only allows it to an installed app, so a dot would stay on for most people. Pictures: `local/data-safety-pictures/message/` (`node local/shoot-keep-message.js`).
- **Asking again quietly:** once the button was tapped (`keepDataWanted`), browsers that decide by themselves are asked again at every start and on `appinstalled`. Not Firefox, which would show its question again.
- **Not tried in Firefox or Safari** (the tests drive only Chromium); the owner should try Firefox.

## Tips

- **One line each, beside what they are about, with an arrow** (`src/tips.js`; the owner, 2026-10-15, after testers didn't read longer tips at the bottom of the screen). Each comes when its thing first matters. The tips in `TIPS`: the grades (`grades`, from `offerGrades`; "How was this card? Choose, or tap the card to take the suggestion.", 2026-10-18), "I know this word" (`lesson`), the character boxes (`verdict`, a word of several characters only), the gestures (`gestures`, below Clear), ☰ (`decks`), the first ready-made level's Add (`ready`), the parts on a word's page (`wordPage`, "Tap a character or part to see more about it.", 2026-10-18).
- **A tip waits until its element can be seen** and is put away while it can't (`targetBox`, using `elementFromPoint`); a timer (`LOOK_EVERY_MS`) follows the element, idle while no tip is up or waiting.
- Screens announce moments with a `screen-shown` event and know nothing of tips. Each tip shows once per device (`tipsSeen`; `tipsOn` switches them all, with "Show all tips again", under "Help, feedback and licenses"; both device settings). The card is a popover moved into the pop-up opened last; tips wait for the introduction and What's new. **The browser tests switch tips off** at start; `tests/browser/tips.browser.js` turns them on.

## The menu and the top bar

- **A top bar with ☰ (`menu-button`) and the screen's name** (`place-title`, `PLACE_TITLES` in `src/main.js`; the owner, 2026-10-14). ☰ opens a panel from the left (`side-menu`): Review, Learn under it while words are waiting (with how many), Decks, Words, Progress (`docs/cards.md`), then Basics of Chinese, Send feedback and **Settings, last at the bottom** (the owner, 2026-10-21: where most apps put it), with the line about a backup due under it. Its buttons keep their old tab IDs (`review-tab`, `decks-tab`, `settings-button`…), which the tests tap. The backup dot is on ☰ and on Settings in the panel.
- **The top bar stays put while the page scrolls** (the owner, 2026-10-17) on every screen without a card up, with a line under it once scrolled (`scrolled`; `main:not(.focused) > .top-bar`). While a card is up, only ☰ (or the way back) is in the corner.
- **The bar is as tall on every screen** (`--top-bar`, 36px); its ways back ("‹ Decks", "‹ Back", "‹ Settings", "‹ Practicing a word") are 44px to tap and spill over above and below.
- **Every arrow in the app is the same drawn path** (`.back-arrow`, `.side-arrow-icon`; the owner, 2026-10-17: the font's "‹" was too small), as heavy as ☰.

## Basics of Chinese

Built on 2026-10-21 (roadmap, near-term; the owner chose where it lives, sound and what it covers that day, after tester 3, who had almost no Chinese, found the tone card confusing).

- **A pop-up of five short screens** (`basics-dialog`, `src/basics.js`), with Back and Next held at the bottom as in the introduction (`.welcome-bar`) and a ✕: **Characters** (one syllable each, most with a meaning; 火 + 山 = 火山; simplified and traditional), **Pinyin** (the letters that don't sound as in English: x, q, c, z, zh, ü, each with ▶; the buzzing i after z, c, s, zh, ch, sh, r), **Tones** (mā má mǎ mà, each with ▶, its mark drawn as on a tone card, and māma for the neutral tone; the change of two third tones), **Writing** (stroke order's rules, hold for a hint, swipe up to start again, writing kept as drawn and shortcuts accepted), **Four kinds of card** (Meaning, Pinyin, Tone with the five marks, Writing; Learn and Review), last because it ends on how studying goes (the owner, 2026-10-21). Pinyin says in a line that its marks are the tones, which come next.
- **Four ways to it** (the owner: the pop-up, linked, and from the introduction): "Basics of Chinese" in the ☰ menu (`basics-button`, first of the bottom group) and in Settings › Help (`settings-basics-button`); "New to Chinese? Start with the basics" on the introduction's first screen (`welcome-basics-button`), opening over it so that closing it comes back to the same screen (the introduction itself is otherwise left alone, the owner, 2026-10-19); and **"What are tones?" on a tone card's face** while asking (`tones-link`, `src/toneFlow.js`), which opens it at the screen on tones (`open('tones')`), only until 3 tone cards have been graded (`docs/cards.md`). Not in Review's screen without words, which already has three buttons (the tester's rule).
- **Characters in the script being learned:** a character the scripts write apart is filled in from `data-simplified`/`data-traditional` when it opens (妈/媽), and every piece of Chinese gets `lang` from `settings.script` (`data-zh`); 学 and 學 are shown side by side as the example of the two.
- **Its sounds are the app's recordings** (`playRecording` in `src/speech.js`), named in `BASICS_RECORDINGS` (`src/basicsSounds.js`; a unit test checks each exists), and **kept with the app's own files** so that it works offline from the start (`docs/offline.md`). There is no neutral-tone recording of ma, so the neutral tone is heard in 妈妈.
- Tested in `tests/browser/basics.browser.js`, the link in `lesson.browser.js`, every screen in `accessibility.browser.js`. Not built: a recording for each example character on the first screen.

## Shared sizes and kinds of button

Agreed on 2026-10-17 (the first step of the pass over the looks, `docs/ux-review.md`).

- **Every size of text, space and corner is named at the top of `src/style.css`**, under the colors. Text: `--size-tiny` 0.8rem (never smaller), `--size-small` 0.9, `--size-body` 1, `--size-large` 1.1, `--size-title` 1.25, `--size-big` 1.5; large characters, numbers and arrows keep their own. Space: `--space-tiny` 4px to `--space-huge` 24px in five steps; 2px hairlines stay. Corners: `--radius` 8px, `--radius-large` 12px, `--radius-round` (fully round ends, the streak pill). Tap heights: `--tap` 48px, `--tap-small` 44px (the owner: yes to 44px), `--tap-large` 56px (the stacked grades on a tablet held sideways).
- **Four kinds of button:** main (`primary`, at most one a screen); the usual one; quiet (`link-button`: underlined text in the text's own color, the owner's choice from pictures, since blue means pinyin and the main button; 44px tall alone, as tall as its text beside other text, with an invisible layer to tap); `danger` (red text and edge) for what removes something: Remove a word or words, Start over selected words, Delete a deck, Replace everything when importing. A word's per-card "Start over" stays plain (a list of red buttons would shout).
- **What is chosen looks the same everywhere** (the owner, 2026-10-17): a tint of blue (`--selected`), a blue edge and bold text, for tabs, the place in the menu and Settings' sidebar.
- **Round buttons** (☰, the loudspeaker, ⚙) are 36px to the eye and 44px to tap through an invisible layer (`button.icon-button::before`); the atlas can't see the layer and lists them as small. A round button needs `min-height: 0`, since every button is at least 48px tall.

## Dark mode and the looks

- **The "Look" setting** (`theme`, a device setting; values `light`, `dark`, `auto`): "Follow device theme" (default), Yutu light, Yutu dark, in Settings › Look and sound and on the introduction's first screen as three samples made from `icons/swatch.png` (as it is, darkened with a CSS filter, and the two cut along the diagonal; radio buttons hidden from sight, not from keyboards and screen readers).
- **Yutu light and Yutu dark** (the owner, 2026-10-17, from pictures of the real screens), colors from the icon: Yutu light is the moon's paper (`--bg` #f1e9d4) with ink (#2a2d29); Yutu dark a dark gray with a hint of green (#1d2120) with the moon's paper (#ece3cb) for text and strokes (the owner took the most subtle of three). Jade (`--accent`) is pinyin, links and what is chosen. **In the dark look the jade is a grayer nephrite (#8db89a) and the tint behind what is chosen (`--selected`) a warm moonlight (#35322a), not green** (the owner, 2026-10-19, draft B of four, from pictures): the first dark jade (#7cc4a3) read as mint. What is chosen is shown by its jade edge; the tint only adds to it. **Main buttons are a moonlit slate blue, not jade** (`--accent-fill`): a jade "Again" under a missed character read as praise. "Passed" is a leaf green, yellower than the jade; "missed" a seal red.
- **Passed and missed are not told by color alone** (2026-10-17, after a red-green color blindness simulation): each judged box has a ✓ or ✕ on its corner (`.slot.passed::after`, `.slot.missed::after`, also on tone cards), and missed is darker than passed in the light look and brighter in the dark (`--danger-bg`, `--reference-missed`). The owner chose this from four and did not want blue and orange. A tone card's answered syllable has the same ✓ or ✕ (`.face-pinyin.passed::after`; 2026-10-18).
- **Every color is named once at the top of `src/style.css`**, a value for each look (`:root`, `:root[data-theme='dark']`, and the dark values again under `prefers-color-scheme`). `src/theme.js` sets `data-theme` (none for "Follow device theme", so the page follows the device before settings are read). Hanzi Writer needs values: `src/quiz.js` reads them with `themeColor` and resets them on `theme-changed`; kept strokes and the grid use `var(--ink)` and `var(--grid)`. The manifest's colors stay light (#f1e9d4); `theme-color` follows the look. Checked in `tests/browser/settings.browser.js`.

## Accessibility

Reviewed on 2026-10-15 at the owner's request; keys 1 to 4 for the grades (`docs/cards.md`). Rules for new code:

- **Chinese is marked as Chinese** with `lang` (`zh-Hant-TW` or `zh-Hans-CN`, `langFor` in `src/lang.js`), or a screen reader uses an English voice and the phone may pick the wrong regional font. Mixed text goes through `fillMixed`. Where the script isn't known (a deck's name, a note), `settings.script` is taken. Not marked: drop-down options and text boxes.
- **Messages are read out:** the status line under the writing area (`#status`, not the stroke count) and the notes after an action have `role="status"`.
- **The menu marks the place shown** with `aria-current="page"`.
- **A cut-off meaning opens by keyboard**: `clampWithPopup` makes it a button while it has more.
- **Contrast:** text 4.5:1 (3:1 when large) in both looks; a field's edge 3:1 (`--field-border`). Green text is `--success-text`; white on red is on `--danger-fill`.
- **Everything tapped is at least 24px** (WCAG 2.2 AA); buttons 44px or more (`--tap-small`). A small button whose row must not grow gets an invisible layer (`::after`) instead of padding; words inside text may be as narrow as the word.
- **`tests/browser/accessibility.browser.js` checks all of this** on each screen in both looks. Add new screens and pop-ups to it.
- **OpenDyslexic as the interface's font** (tester 4, 2026-10-21; the owner chose OpenDyslexic, 2026-10-22): Settings › Look and sound › Font, "The device's own" (default) or "OpenDyslexic" (`font`, a learner's setting, not the device's). `src/font.js` loads the two files (`public/fonts/opendyslexic/`, SIL OFL 1.1, README there) with `FontFace` only once chosen, and sets `data-font="dyslexic"` on `<html>`, which the style sheet follows. **Only Latin-1 letters and punctuation** (`unicodeRange`): its tone marks were drawn badly (wǒmen's caron floated off), so pinyin and Chinese stay in the device's font. **Drawn at 88% with the device font's line heights** (`sizeAdjust`, `ascentOverride`…): as it is, its wide letters and tall lines wrapped the rows above the writing area and moved it down by up to 160px on a small phone; so it moves 15 to 40px in a lesson (the lesson's note and "I know this word" take two lines), and Settings and the introduction grow longer. Pictures: `local/tester4/opendyslexic-*.png`. Checked in `tests/browser/settings.browser.js`.
- Not done: checking by hand with VoiceOver or TalkBack. Writing itself needs drawing, by the nature of the app.

## Interface text and translations

Started on 2026-10-22 (the owner: "prepare the app for translation"; the plan in `docs/goals.md`, "Translations", layer 1). **English, Italian and German** (`src/text/it.js`, `de.js`; 2026-10-22, the owner): first drafts by Claude, released on 2026-10-22 and to be checked by a native speaker of each (a tester for Italian; German still to find). How they are written, and the words used for the app's ideas: `docs/translations.md`.

- **Every piece of interface text is in `src/text/en.js`**, a flat list of keys (`'settings.script': 'Script'`), grouped by screen. **Code asks for text with `t(key, values)`** (`src/text.js`), never writes English itself. `{name}` in a text is a gap filled from `values`; a number put in a gap is written as the language writes numbers. A text that changes with a number is given as its forms (`{ one: '{count} word', other: '{count} words' }`), chosen by the browser's `Intl.PluralRules`. Dates and numbers go through `formatDate`, `formatDateTime`, `formatNumber`, never a fixed `'en-US'`.
- **The page's own text** (`index.html`) is marked, not written: `data-t="key"` fills an element's text, `data-t-html` its text with markup (links, `<b>`, `<br>`, Chinese with its `lang`), `data-t-title`, `data-t-aria-label`, `data-t-placeholder`, `data-t-alt` an attribute. `applyText()` fills them once, at the top of `src/main.js`, and sets `<html lang>`. Text beside a control in a label goes in a `<span data-t>` of its own, since filling the label would remove the control. A sentence with a box in the middle (the streak's goal) is in pieces, which a translator may need to change.
- **The language belongs to the device and is kept in `localStorage`** (`language`, `language-test` on the test copy), not the database: it must be known before anything is shown. **Until one is chosen, the app follows the browser's own list of languages** (the first it has; "it-CH" counts as Italian), else English. Chosen at the top of Settings › Look and sound (`language-select`, filled from `LANGUAGE_NAMES` in `src/text.js`: each language named in itself, with its own `lang`). Changing it starts the app again (`setLanguage`; with no language, back to following the browser), so the text never changes while the app runs; a module may look its text up when it loads. Every language's file is in the bundle (about 100 KB each, read once; offline there is nothing to fetch).
- **The test language** (Settings › For testing, `pseudo-language-checkbox`; the owner, 2026-10-22): every text in look-alike letters and a third longer, "[Šéţţîñĝš ··]" (`src/text/pseudo.js`; app makers call it pseudo-localization). Text in plain English has not gone through `t()`; a screen that breaks shows where a longer language won't fit. Pictures: `node local/text-pictures/shoot.js`. It found the list in Sentences › Writing pushing past the panel's edge (fixed: `min-inline-size: 0` on the fieldset).
- **Checks** (`tests/text.test.js`): every key asked for (by `t('…')` or `data-t…`) is in `en.js`; every key in `en.js` is used (a key kept in a list counts if it is quoted in the code: keep keys whole, never built from pieces); forms for numbers always have `other`; `data-t` text has no markup; nothing on the page is written in English outside the `data-t…` attributes. `tests/browser/text.browser.js` checks the filling in and the switch. **A translation** is `src/text/<language>.js` with the same keys; a key it lacks shows in English, and `node scripts/missing-text.js <language>` lists what is missing or out of date. **A new or changed English text** needs its Italian and German too (`docs/translations.md`); the check above runs for both.
- **Everything is moved over** (2026-10-22): `tests/text.test.js` checks the whole page, and no English is left in the code outside `src/text/en.js` except: deck names (`decks.js`: saved with each deck, so a later language would not change them), the specialist fields matched in the dictionary's English (`dictionary.js`), Anki's own field names, browser names, the details about the device in an error report or feedback email (`aboutThisDevice` in `errors.js`, for whoever fixes the app), and past What's new entries (`changes.js`). **Code never acts on a message's wording:** `checkWord` (`words.js`) and the Anki import give a `reason` beside each `problem` (`pinyinMissing`, `noPinyin`, `sentenceNoChinese`…). A plain series is joined by `formatList` (the separator in the text, `list.separator`; not `Intl.ListFormat`, whose plain series differs from language to language), one with "and" before its last item by `formatAndList` (`list.and`). Text the code used to lowercase ("Practice · no mistakes") has a key of its own, since lowercasing breaks German nouns; ordinals ("2nd try") use `{ count, ordinal: true }`; week days in Progress come from `Intl`, Monday first. Some sentences are still built from pieces around a control or a deck's name (the streak's goal in Settings, "Add [reading] to your words, in [deck]" on a word's page, "; 3 you already had" after adding a ready-made level): a translator may need to reorder them in code.
- **Not part of this:** dictionary meanings (no dictionary in Italian or German: the owner, 2026-10-22), learning notes, TOCFL topic and radical names (`docs/goals.md`, layers 2 to 4).
