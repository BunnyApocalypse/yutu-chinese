# Decks

Built on 2026-10-13 at the owner's request; answers marked "owner" were given to questions put to them.

## The Decks tab

- **Two sections**, chosen at the top: "My decks" and "Ready-made decks" (owner). Coming to the tab always shows My decks' front page, with no deck opened by itself (the owner, 2026-10-14: an opened "Chinese 中文" misled new learners); this is reset before anything is waited for, so a quick tap isn't undone. `openDeckId` in `src/wordsPanel.js` says which page is open.
- **The front page:** the decks inside no other, "+ New deck" beside the heading (`new-deck-dialog`; makes the deck inside the one whose page is open), and all words with the search box. A tip beside the first deck's › says what a tap does (`deckRows`).
- **Decks are listed in the learner's own order** (the owner, 2026-10-31; from 2026-10-14 most recently active first): the tree's order (`deckTree`), by each deck's `order`, then name. A new deck goes last (`newDeckRecord`), the ready-made levels after the decks made before them. Choosers have the same order. The browser tests open decks by name: `openDeckPage` (with its ›) and `openLearn` in the harness.
- **Edit beside the heading** (`edit-decks-button`; the owner, 2026-10-31: "there should be an edit mode"), on the front page and "Decks inside this one": each row becomes the deck's name and counts with ↑, ↓ (`.deck-move-up`, `.deck-move-down`) and ⚙ (`.deck-settings`, the same Deck settings as on its page: rename, move inside another, daily goal, delete). Tapping the deck itself does nothing while editing. ↑ and ↓ swap it with the deck beside it and number those side by side afresh from 0 (`movedDeckOrder` in `src/decks.js`; only the decks whose `order` changed are saved, stamped like any change). "Done", another page or coming back to the tab ends it (`editing`, `editingIn`, `stopEditing` in `src/decksPanel.js`).
- **A tap on a deck's row studies it** (the owner, 2026-10-16, after a tester found "way too many buttons"): Learn while any of its words wait, Review once all are learned, its page if it has no words (`mainAction` in `src/decksPanel.js`); the row says "Tap to learn", "Tap to review" or "Tap to add words" (`.deck-tap`). **› opens the deck's page** (`.deck-open`).
- **Studying a deck from Decks has "‹ Decks" in the top bar** (`study-back-button`, `studiedFrom` in `src/main.js`; tester 2's R8, 2026-10-18), back to where it was left; "‹ Words" when it came from Words. **While a card is up, the arrow alone replaces ☰** (the owner's idea; `main.focused .top-bar:has(#study-back-button:not([hidden]))`), "Back to Decks" to a screen reader.
- **A deck's page:** "‹ Decks" or "‹ <parent>" in the top bar (`deck-back-button`); "Decks › Textbook › Chapter 2" (`renderPath`) only inside another deck; the name with ⚙ for Deck settings; the counts; **one main button**, "Learn new words" with "Review" small beside it, or "Review this deck" once all are learned (the owner, 2026-10-16); the decks inside it; "+ Add to deck"; and its words, those of decks inside it marked "in Chapter 1". **A sentence or radical says so** at the row's end ("sentence · waiting"; `kind` in `renderList`; tester 2 took 我們是同學。 for 我們 added twice).
- **The way back in the top bar is decided in one place** (`renderDecks`): only while a deck's page is on screen, and the screen's name is left alone while Decks and Words aren't showing. Deciding it elsewhere once left "‹ Decks" over Learn, and "Learn" over Review's own way back.
- **Each deck shows how far it is learned** (the owner, 2026-10-13): "512 words · 37 learned (7%)" with a thin bar (`countsOf`, `fillBar`). "Learned" is `isLearned`, so 100% means every word has been through Learn, not that it is known. Rounded down.

## Adding

- **One panel, "+ Add to deck"** (`add-dialog`; the owner, 2026-10-16, from screenshots): "Add to Chinese 中文" with three tabs, One word, A list of words, A sentence (`showAddForm`). Beside "Words in this deck", or the main button on a deck without words. Ready-made levels aren't offered there. Tests pick a form with `useAddForm`, the panel left closed.
- **"A list of words": choose, see, add** (`src/addListForm.js`; the owner, 2026-11-08, from mockups in `local/list-mockups/`: "Check list" wasn't seen as the next step, and a file had no business in a text box). At the start a one-line hint (`addList.lead`), **Choose a file** as the main button, "Or paste a list" (`list-paste-button`) for the box, and the format details folded under "What can the file look like?" (`list-format`: example lines, `addList.examples`, and the columns as the settings ask, `list-hint`). **No Check button:** a chosen file is read at once and shown by its name with "Choose another file" (`list-file-line`), never poured into the box; a pasted list is read once typing pauses (`READ_AFTER_MS`, 600 ms; a reading overtaken by more typing is dropped, `readings`). Then (`list-found`, `data-state` `reading`/`read`, which the tests wait for, `pasteList` in the harness): "2,000 words: 1,952 new, 48 already yours." (`addList.found`), the notes (same characters with another reading; columns swapped), the lines that couldn't be read under their count in red (`list-problems-fold`, open up to 5, `PROBLEMS_SHOWN_OPEN`), the first 5 words as rows of the Words list (`list-preview`, `PREVIEW_ROWS`) and "and 1,995 more", and one main button naming the deck, "Add 1,952 words to Chinese 中文" (or "Put 3 words in …" when all are there already). After it (`list-done`): what was added and "Add another list". Opening another deck's page starts afresh (`forget`). Pictures as built: `local/list-pictures/` (`node local/list-pictures/shoot.js`).
- **Words go into the deck whose page is open.** A word already there is put in this deck as well and keeps its meaning; a list with nothing new can still be "put in this deck".
- **"Learn it now" after adding a word** (the owner, 2026-10-16): under the note in the add panel while the word waits, until the next is typed; and as a bar at the top of a word's page right after adding it from the dictionary ("Added to Chinese 中文", `page-learn-now`). It puts the word first in line and opens Learn on that deck (`learnNow`). Not for lists or sentences.

## How decks work

- **Every word is in at least one deck** (owner). The app starts with "Chinese 中文" (`DEFAULT_DECK`, fixed ID `chinese`, renamable). `homeForEveryWord` puts words in no existing deck into it at every start (`fileListWordsInDecks` in `src/main.js`), making it if no deck exists, so code can rely on `word.decks` after start-up.
- **A word can be in several decks and has one schedule** (owner). `decks` lists deck IDs; studying a deck only narrows which words come up. A deck includes those inside it, to any depth (`deckWithInside`). A deck can't be put inside itself or its own inside (`canBeInside`).
- **A word's pop-up panel has a box per deck** in a fold-out, at least one ticked; "Remove" there removes the word altogether.
- **Studying a deck:** the chooser at the top of Review and Learn, left out with one deck, sharing a line with the counts; or a deck page's buttons (`studyDeck` callback in `src/main.js`). Learn studies one deck (the `studyDeck` setting, synced since 2026-11-08); **Review has its own list, `reviewDecks`** (synced; started from `studyDeck`), set by "Review this deck" or a tap reviewing a deck to that deck alone.
- **Review ticks as many decks as wanted** (the owner, 2026-11-10; Learn later, not built): the panel has tickboxes and "Done" (`deck-choice-done`, held at the panel's foot), titled "Review which decks?" (or "Review what?" with "简体字 cards" first, which is ticked like a deck, `docs/cards.md`). A deck ticked includes the decks inside it, which show ticked and greyed; ticking it unticks those inside it it had (`tick` in `src/deckChooser.js`), so to leave out one deck inside, untick the deck around it and tick the others (the owner's choice over half-ticked decks). "All words" is ticked when nothing else is, and ticking it unticks the rest; it is **the first row** (the owner, 2026-11-10, rather than an "Unselect all" button), then "简体字 cards", then "Your words from" over the decks (Learn keeps the forms first). **Every row says how many are due** ("20 due", due and new together, as "简体字 cards" did; the owner, 2026-11-10: one row alone with a number looked odd): a deck counts its words' cards and those of the decks inside it (`dueNotes` in `src/review.js`, from `planSession`'s `cardsByWord`), worked out as the panel opens (the `notes` option of `createDeckChooser`), not at every card. A panel opened again before the browser's late "close" first chooses what the last one had ticked (`finishClosedPanel`). The choice is made as the panel closes, however it is closed, and starts Review afresh as choosing a deck did. The button and the card's top line say the deck's name, "3 decks" or "简体字 cards + 2 decks" (`review.decksTicked`, `review.entryAndDecks`). `reviewChoice` in `src/review.js` works out the decks with those inside them; with several ticked, Review's limit for new cards is the one in Settings, not a deck's. Tests choose with `tickDecks` in the harness.
- **The chooser is a panel of the app's own, the decks in a tree that folds** (`src/deckChooser.js`; the owner, 2026-10-31, from a picture of the phone's drop-down with every Skritter section on a line of its own with its whole path; chosen over leaving out decks two levels deep). A button naming the deck by its own name with ▾ (`deck-choice-button`, its full path for a screen reader) opens `deck-choice-dialog`: "All words" and the decks inside no other, each deck by its own name, indented, with › to show the decks inside it (`.deck-fold`, `aria-expanded`); the branch of the deck chosen starts open. A tap on a deck chooses it and closes the panel. The browser tests choose with `studyDeck` in the harness.
- **While a card is up the deck is on the card's top line** (`deck-tag`; the owner, 2026-10-31: the line with the chooser is put away while a card is up, so the deck could not be changed until nothing was left; a place in the menu was tried and not kept, "it doesn't belong in the sidebar"). Between the kind of card and Show pinyin, quiet, its name the one thing cut with "…" where the line is short. Changing the deck there shows a card of the new deck. **In Learn** (the owner, 2026-10-31, chose this of four places) it is in the column left of today's words, after the corner's button: the name with ▾ where the line is at least 480px wide (a tablet; `@container` on `.type-row`), else a picture of a deck (`.deck-tag-icon`), as the gap on a phone is about 44px. "All words" keeps the queue's order. If the studied deck is deleted, all words are studied.
- **A deck can have its own "New words per day"** (`dailyGoal`; owner): Learn's goal while it is studied, and Review's limit for cards to write again. Not inherited from the deck it is inside.
- **Deleting a deck asks what becomes of its words** (owner), in Deck settings; decks inside it go too. Words also in another deck stay there. For the rest: "Delete the deck, keep the words" moves them up one level, or to "Chinese 中文" (made again if needed); or "Delete the deck and remove N words". If the deck is the only place left for them, they can only be removed or moved first. A deleted deck leaves a mark in `removed` with `kind: 'deck'`.
- **Every other place a deck is chosen uses the same panel** (the owner, 2026-11-01): the Words tab's deck (`words-deck-select`), Select words' deck to put them in (`bulk-deck`), Deck settings' "Inside" (`deck-parent`, the panel headed "Inside which deck?"), and on a word's page the deck for "Add to my words" and for its example sentence (`deckChooser` in `src/wordPage.js`). `deckButtonFor` in `src/deckChooser.js` puts a button in the drop-down list's place (its ID the list's with `-button`) and hides the list, which still holds the choice: it is filled by `fillDeckSelect` (each entry says the deck's own name and the deck it is inside, `data-name`, `data-parent`), its value is read and its `change` heard as before, and the browser tests still choose with `choose`. The panel's heading is the list's name ("Deck whose words are listed"); a deck whose parent isn't offered (a ready-made group on a word's page) is listed at the top with its whole path. One panel, `deck-choice-dialog`, serves all choosers.
- **Every deck chooser leaves out the groups inside ready-made levels** (`fillDeckSelect`, and the same in `deckChooser.js`; 2026-10-14 and 15), except a group that is the one chosen (`keep`). The groups stay on the Decks tab.

## Ready-made decks

- **Each HSK and TOCFL level is a deck of its own, inside no other** ("HSK 1 (3.0)", "TOCFL Novice 1"; the owner, 2026-10-14: a level takes months), after the decks made before it (`levelName`, `LEVEL_ORDER_BASE`). The radicals keep "Radicals 部首" with Common and Uncommon inside. `flattenedReadyMadeDecks` moved older devices' levels out of their list's deck; deck IDs, and so word IDs, didn't change. Details of the lists: `docs/word-lists.md`.
- **The ready-made decks stay as their lists have them** (the owner, 2026-10-18): nothing puts words in or out of one except on its own page. Select words' bar offers only the learner's own decks, and has no Move on a ready-made deck's page (`readyMadeDeck` in `src/cardsPanel.js`). A word's panel has boxes only for the learner's decks, names the ready-made ones under them ("Also in the ready-made deck TOCFL Novice 1 › Family.") and keeps them (`decksAfter` in `src/wordDialog.js`). "+ Add to deck" stays on a ready-made deck's page.
- **A word's example sentence goes into the word's group to begin with** (`deckChooser` in `src/wordPage.js`): the chooser offers the word's ready-made decks (group, else level) with their path, and the learner's own; for a word only in the learner's decks, the first of them.
