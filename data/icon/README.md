# The app icon's artwork

The pictures `scripts/icon.html` puts together into the app's icons (`node scripts/make-icons.js` draws them into `public/icons/`). They are not under the GPL that covers the app's code: each keeps its own license, below. All three licenses ask for credit, given in the app's "Credits and licenses" (`credits.appIcon` in `src/text/`).

## The 兔: `tu-sheet.png`, `tu.png`, `speck-1.png` to `speck-4.png`

Written for this app by the calligrapher **南蠻大熊**, commissioned by the owner (2026). Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) (CC BY 4.0).

- `tu-sheet.png`: the sheet as delivered, three 兔 in black ink on a transparent ground.
- `tu.png`: the left one, cut out of the sheet (the owner's choice, 2026-11-11).
- `speck-1.png` to `speck-4.png`: four tiny specks of ink from the same sheet, used as the stars.

## The paper: `washi.jpg`

A square of the cream sheet in [Washi(Sugihara paper).JPG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Washi(Sugihara_paper).JPG) by Tomomarusan, on Wikimedia Commons, licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 2.5](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/) (CC BY 2.5; the photographer also offers it under other licenses). Changed: a 250-pixel square cut from the cream sheet, enlarged to 512 pixels and made 12% brighter.

## The night sky: `ink-wash.jpg`

From the inside of [Ink-stain-texture-15.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ink-stain-texture-15.jpg) by designshard (from [Flickr](https://www.flickr.com/photos/28920179@N03/3452034733/)), on Wikimedia Commons, licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/) (CC BY 2.0). Changed: a 290-pixel square cut from inside the blue stain, a small white spot covered with the wash beside it, enlarged to 512 pixels, and its lightness turned into the night sky's dark greens.
